Execution playbook

  1. Have the bank identify its recorded account-holder category and the exact lawful source of each CNY deposit. Do not relabel salary, a gift, property proceeds or company funds as one another. Measures Arts. 2, 5, 28
  2. For lawful current-account income, assemble passport, amount-linked source documents and Chinese tax evidence; request review under Detailed Rules Art. 13 rather than assuming an annual quota is the only option. Detailed Rules Art. 13
  3. If work and tax history qualify, request a Wise CNY-to-own-USD-account quote; verification establishes the overall permitted amount. Provider transaction caps do not authorize quota evasion. [S11] Art. 7
  4. For a bank quote, obtain CNY total debit, USD net receipt, the selling rate, each sending/intermediary/receiving charge, and quote expiry. The existing tariff/model page is a checklist, not an executable offer. [S4] [S5]
  5. If someone calls a charge a tax, obtain the taxing authority, tax base, tax year and assessment. Evaluate US worldwide income and eligible foreign tax credits separately from provider charges. IRS income IRS credit
  6. Review prior and current FBAR and Form 8938 obligations independently of the wire amount. Moving funds does not establish that earlier reporting was complete. [S25] [S27]
  7. Submit only authentic, consistent documents through the approved bank/provider flow; retain the FX purchase record, wire receipt and final account credit. Do not split to evade review. Measures Arts. 5–8
