Sources

Every rule, rate and penalty figure used on this site, with what it establishes and what happened when it was retrieved on 2026-11-08. The authoritative language is Chinese wherever SAFE publishes both, because SAFE's own English pages state that the Chinese text prevails.

How the verification was done

  1. Each primary text was opened and read, not summarised from a search result. The article numbers quoted on the site are the article numbers in the text.
  2. Where a figure is computed — every number on the Costs page — the inputs are one bank's published rate and tariff, and the formula is printed on the page.
  3. Where a figure could not be sourced, the cell is blank and the item appears on Flagged.
  4. Superseded texts (the 1997 FX Regulations) are listed separately and used only to explain an outdated number, never to state current law.
