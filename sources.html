What counts as official here. A source is official if it is published by the regulator (SAFE, PBOC, Customs, the State Taxation Administration, the IRS, FinCEN, CBP), by the legislature or government, or — for bank charges — by the regulated institution itself in its own published tariff. Travel sites, accountancy blogs, news articles and commercial bank marketing pages are used for nothing. Every date below is the date the page was actually fetched and read.

Two entries carry caveats rather than a clean verification: an IRS page that is official but out of date, and a Customs reply that confirms a figure whose primary instrument could not be reached. Both are labelled in the status column and cross-referenced on the flagged page.
