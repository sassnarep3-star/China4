Reproducibility

How to audit the calculations

  1. Open scripts/build_site.py and confirm the Decimal inputs BUY=6.6648, SELL=6.6929 and amount ladder $10,000…$400,000.
  2. Run python3 scripts/build_site.py. It rebuilds the HTML and data/cost-model.json without network calls.
  3. Compare BOC commission against the CNY 50 minimum and CNY 260 maximum; add CNY 150 telecom only to TT, not draft.
  4. Verify the half-spread from BOC’s same-timestamp buy/sell midpoint. Do not substitute the PBOC fixing or a later mid-market quote.
  5. Because BOC’s board rotates, re-query the dated quote through its official historical search (date 2026-11-09, currency USD, captcha) rather than expecting the row to remain on a page number.
  6. Check current Wise quotes directly; no current price is inferred from its 2024 examples.
  7. Confirm that ATM amounts above $10,000 are marked infeasible under the annual cap and that unknown values remain blank.

The dashboard intentionally does not fetch live prices during a visit. This prevents a stale static page from presenting new values without an audit trail.
