ATM and cash carrying, with caps and declarations.

Overseas ATM withdrawal with a mainland card verified route

Withdraw local cash abroad using a mainland card, subject to SAFE's per-person annual and per-card daily caps. Unsuitable for large balances.

Eligibility: Cardholder using own mainland card abroad; no borrowing/lending cards. BOC international-debit tariff is the published example.

Limits: CNY 100,000 equivalent per person per calendar year across all domestic cards; CNY 10,000 equivalent per card per day. Exceeding annual cap suspends overseas withdrawals for current and following year.

Pros

  • Small cash access while traveling
  • Published BOC international-debit fee formula
  • No SWIFT paperwork for small withdrawals

Cons

  • Annual cap far below large targets
  • ATM surcharge and FX unknown
  • Cash handling/declaration risk

Purchase FX cash and carry it across the border conditional

Lawful only within China export/permit rules and US declaration rules. Not a practical large-balance route.

Eligibility: Lawful FX purchase and export permit where required; China customs declaration and US FinCEN 105 when triggered.

Limits: China RMB cash reference CNY 20,000; FX cash USD 5,000 declaration reference; USD 5,000–10,000 bank permit band; >USD 10,000 generally requires SAFE permit and is not ordinary. US >USD 10,000 aggregate family/group declaration.

Pros

  • Physical immediate access for small amounts
  • BOC permit fee waived through 2026-12-31 in current tariff

Cons

  • Permit/declaration complexity
  • Loss/theft and deposit risk
  • FX cash spread unknown
  • Not suitable for USD 10,000–400,000 targets
