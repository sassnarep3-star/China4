Most important answer first

Start with the actual source of the CNY. A foreign individual with documented lawful China earnings can usually use a bank or an eligible licensed salary-remittance service without treating USD 50,000 as a universal outbound cap. Property, inheritance, migration and business proceeds need their own evidence and tax analysis. Compare same-time, all-in net-USD quotes. Do not assume that moving principal creates no currency gain or that Chinese tax eliminates US tax.

The lawful starting point

Match the route to the actual source of the CNY. For documented China salary or other lawful current-account income, start with a bank or an eligible own-account salary service and compare same-time all-in net-USD quotes. Do not treat the USD 50,000 facilitation quota as a universal outbound cap, and do not assume “already taxed in China” means “no US tax.”
