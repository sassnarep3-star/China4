Moving money from China to the US

Executive summary · A decision guide for a US citizen with funds in a Chinese bank.

Start with the documented bank route

Ask the Chinese bank to classify the source of the funds and quote a compliant CNY-to-USD conversion and outward wire to your own US account. For legitimate China-sourced current-account income, SAFE says foreign individuals can purchase foreign exchange and remit it, subject to the applicable requirements. Whether this account qualifies is unknown. SAFE 2025 (Chinese)

There is no verified universal tax-free shortcut or cheapest provider. An unpaid income tax may relate to how funds were earned, not be a tax on the wire. Conversion itself can generate a separate US foreign-currency gain. IRS Pub. 54 · IRS FX guidance

Practical sequence

  1. Identify the source: wages, savings, property sale, investment, inheritance or something else. Assemble bank statements, identity, evidence of source and tax records where applicable.
  2. Ask the bank for its applicable category, documentary requirements, written rate, outgoing/intermediary/receiving fees and whether a USD wire or CNY wire is supported.
  3. Have a qualified adviser assess Chinese tax already owed, US past filings, FX basis and possible credits before converting. Do not use other people’s quotas to evade controls. SAFE enforcement
  4. Compare written net proceeds and preserve records of conversion, fees and filings.
Research, not legal or tax advice. This is not a bank quote or a determination of your eligibility. Verify current rules and your documents with the bank and qualified China–US advisers before acting. Sources reviewed 8 November 2026; older guidance may have changed.
