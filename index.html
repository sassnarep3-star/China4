Executive answer · independently corrected

Use the route your documents support—not a quota workaround.

For a US passport holder, the most verifiable default is a licensed-bank conversion and own-account international wire supported by lawful source-of-funds and tax evidence. The $50,000 convenience purchase rule is written for domestic individuals; it is not blanket “ID-only” permission for a foreign individual.

01 · Eligibility first

Four checks before comparing prices

1

Classify identity

SAFE’s implementation measures define a passport-holding foreign citizen as a foreign individual. Confirm which identity the bank has on file. [S2]

2

Classify money

After-tax current-account income, old foreign-currency savings, original-currency reconversion, inheritance and property proceeds follow different rules. [S1] [S20] [S21]

3

Build evidence

Passport, contracts/payslips, amount-matched records and Chinese tax evidence; keep the complete chain from income to bank balance. [S1]

4

Compare net USD

Record CNY principal, execution rate, every sender/intermediary/receiver fee, and final USD credited. Never treat “no fee” as “no spread.” [S4] [S15]

04 · Compliance

The reporting layer does not disappear after conversion

A

FBAR

Aggregate maximum foreign-account values, not just the amount transferred. Due April 15 with automatic extension to October 15. [S25]

B

Income and asset forms

Form 8938 has separate residence/status thresholds. Form 3520 is for specified foreign gifts/bequests and trusts—not moving your own savings. [S27] [S28]

C

Relief, not concealment

Review FEIE versus foreign tax credit for underlying income and correct qualifying past nonwillful filings through IRS procedures. [S33] [S34] [S36] [S37]

Research, not individualized legal, tax, or investment advice. Eligibility follows the source and character of funds, identity used at the bank, tax residence, and transaction purpose.
