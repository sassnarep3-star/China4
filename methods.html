Conversion methods

Each route is conditional. A bank’s ability to send a payment is not proof that a specific balance can be remitted.

Routes and trade-offs

Bank: remit eligible CNY and convert abroadA possible quote to request; conversion and transfer are separate stages.Availability, permitted purpose, receiving-bank acceptance, rates and fees unverified. Do not assume cross-border RMB is available.SAFE 2007

Legitimate China current-account income of an overseas individualSAFE says purchased FX can be remitted, deposited in own FX account or carried out under applicable rules.Classification, necessary documentation and any outstanding tax are fact-specific; no blanket cap or tax exemption inferred.SAFE 2025 (Chinese)
Reconvert previously exchanged foreign currencyAsk the bank whether original exchange receipts permit reconversion.Eligibility, deadlines and amount for this account unverified; this is not an assumption that all RMB balances qualify.SAFE 2007
Property sale, inheritance or investment proceedsAsk bank and local SAFE to identify the applicable capital-account or other procedure.No general conversion entitlement, processing time, tax rate or required documents verified for these facts.SAFE 2007
Chinese bank card: overseas ATM withdrawalMay provide cash overseas if card and network permit.SAFE’s 2017 notice describes RMB 100,000 annual aggregate per individual and RMB 10,000 daily per card; confirm current applicability, network and fees. Not equivalent to a bank wire.SAFE 2017
