1. Lawful current-account CNY income → BOC USD telegraphic transferPrimary

For a foreign individual, SAFE Article 13 supports purchasing FX from lawful current-account RMB income with ID, amount-based evidence and tax records. This is not the domestic-individual ID-only convenience route.

Advantages

  • Clear regulatory basis and complete paper trail
  • One transfer can exceed $50,000 when evidence supports it
  • Published BOC tariff caps the percentage commission at CNY 260

Constraints

  • Bank decides whether documents are sufficient
  • Execution FX, correspondent and recipient charges can change net proceeds
  • Underlying income tax must be addressed separately

Procedure

  1. Confirm the bank records you as a foreign individual and ask its cross-border desk to pre-review the exact source-of-funds category.
  2. Assemble passport, employment/service contract, payslips or transaction documents, bank statements and amount-matched Chinese tax evidence.
  3. Obtain your US account’s legal name, account number, receiving SWIFT/BIC, bank address and incoming-wire instructions; do not guess an intermediary.
  4. Ask BOC for a written CNY principal, USD selling rate, commission, CNY 150 telecom charge, full-arrival option and expected recipient deductions.
  5. Submit the purchase/remittance application through BOC’s cross-border-remittance channel or branch and answer authenticity review consistently.
  6. Save the application, FX memo, SWIFT receipt, fee receipts and final US credit; reconcile them to FBAR and tax workpapers.
Cost treatment

Known model: clamp(0.1% × CNY principal, ¥50, ¥260) + ¥150 + same-quote half-spread. Results: $10k $53.45; $400k $902.72. Add optional full-arrival and receiving charges as applicable.

2. Existing USD savings → international wire (no CNY conversion)Conditional

SAFE Article 15 permits a foreign individual to remit existing FX savings abroad with ID. This route applies only if the balance is already USD (or another currency accepted for the remittance), not to CNY merely described as savings.

Advantages

  • No new CNY→USD purchase or retail conversion spread
  • Simple evidence chain if the FX balance origin is clear

Constraints

  • Does not itself convert the CNY balance
  • Bank can still request authenticity/source documents
  • Wire and receiving charges remain

Procedure

  1. Verify the account currency and whether it is an FX savings account.
  2. Present ID and the bank’s requested evidence for the existing balance.
  3. Follow steps 3–6 of the telegraphic-transfer route, selecting remittance of existing FX rather than a new CNY purchase.
  4. Retain the original deposit/conversion history so the no-new-conversion treatment is auditable.
Cost treatment

BOC wire-only known subtotal: $10k $32.41; $400k $61.26. No half-spread is modeled because no new conversion occurs; correspondent/receiver charges are extra.

3. Unused RMB from an earlier FX conversion → original-currency reconversionConditional

A foreign individual may reconvert unused RMB using ID and the original exchange memo, generally within 24 months. Small ID-only exceptions exist, but they do not make the whole bank balance eligible.

Advantages

  • Direct statutory category for genuinely unused converted funds
  • Can avoid trying to relabel the balance as salary

Constraints

  • Original memo and 24-month timing constrain the amount
  • Not available for unrelated CNY income
  • Bank must match the reconversion to the original transaction

Procedure

  1. Locate each original exchange memo and its date/amount.
  2. Calculate the still-unused portion; exclude unrelated deposits.
  3. Ask the original bank to verify the 24-month window and any small ID-only exception rather than assuming it.
  4. Purchase the original currency within the eligible amount and either hold it as FX or remit using the wire procedure.
  5. Keep the original and new memos linked in your records.
Cost treatment

Use the BOC conversion/wire model only for the eligible amount. The matrix does not imply the entire requested row qualifies.

4. Wise from CNY → own USD account (bank-app or Alipay funding)Compare quote

Wise’s current official guide says eligible non-mainland-Chinese users who work or worked in China and have qualifying Chinese tax records can send from CNY to an account they own. A PBOC-regulated partner handles the China leg.

Advantages

  • Documented non-bank alternative
  • Own-name requirement reduces third-party misuse
  • Published caps and transparent quote flow

Constraints

  • Not for mainland-Chinese senders
  • Tax records within Wise’s accepted period and overall limit control eligibility
  • Current fees/rates are quote-dependent; historical matrix is not an offer

Procedure

  1. Create a personal Wise account and select CNY→USD to your own USD bank account or Wise account.
  2. Upload passport, an accepted current/former work-eligibility document and Chinese personal-income-tax records meeting Wise’s recency rules.
  3. Let Wise calculate your overall sending limit; cancel unfunded transfers you do not intend to complete so their reserved limit is released.
  4. Choose Chinese bank-app funding (up to CNY 300,000 per transfer) or Alipay funding (up to CNY 50,000 per transfer) and record the live fee/rate before paying.
  5. For bank funding, send CNY only from your same-name account to the unique partner safeguarding-account details shown by Wise; for Alipay, complete the displayed payment flow.
  6. Track verification/payout and save the quote, local debit, tax records and final USD receipt.
Cost treatment

Dated 2024 bank-funding formula modeled at $66.96 for $10k and $2,524.64 for $400k (9 transfers at the CNY 300,000/transfer cap); current quote —. Historical Alipay formula is separate and needs far more transfers at the CNY 50,000 cap.

5. BOC cross-border demand draft → US bank collection/depositNiche

BOC’s current personal price list covers both telegraphic transfers and drafts. A draft avoids the CNY 150 telecom charge but adds actual postage and US foreign-item acceptance/collection considerations.

Advantages

  • Same CNY 50–260 commission scale as BOC wire
  • No BOC international telecom charge

Constraints

  • Physical loss/delay and stop-payment risk
  • US bank may treat it as a foreign collection item with delays/fees
  • Postage and receiving terms are not uniform

Procedure

  1. Complete the same eligibility and document pre-review as a BOC wire.
  2. Before ordering, give your US bank the draft’s currency, issuer and amount; obtain written acceptance, endorsement, collection and fee instructions.
  3. Ask BOC for the draft fee and actual postage; make it payable exactly as the US bank instructs.
  4. Purchase/issue the USD draft and record its number, FX memo and receipt.
  5. Use a delivery method you consider adequately trackable/insured, then deposit under the US bank’s foreign-item procedure.
  6. Reconcile final credit and retain collection/fee records.
Cost treatment

Known subtotal excluding postage and US collection: $10k $31.04; $400k $880.31. A dash in the matrix means those charges are unknown.

6. Western Union through an official China partner bankQuote needed

Western Union lists online/mobile and in-person sending from China through partner banks. Its official disclosure says it also makes money from FX and that fees/rates vary by brand, channel and location.

Advantages

  • Official China outbound process
  • Cash pickup can avoid a recipient bank account
  • MTCN tracking and broad agent network

Constraints

  • No current static China→US fee/FX schedule verified
  • Agent, payment, delivery and receiving limits vary
  • SAFE source-of-funds review still applies

Procedure

  1. Use only a partner/channel listed by Western Union and confirm that your US passport and own-name US payout are accepted.
  2. Present ID and the source/tax evidence required for the underlying purchase/remittance.
  3. Request the complete quote: CNY debit, transfer fee, CNY→USD rate, USD payout, agent limit and any cash-payout restriction.
  4. Compare final USD with the BOC and Wise quotes; pay only after the quote is shown.
  5. Keep the receipt/MTCN; the receiver presents required ID and payout details.
  6. Retain the FX and fee evidence for account and tax records.
Cost treatment

Current fee and FX margin are blank in all 40 rows. Expired promotional schedules are not used as current prices.

7. MoneyGram through Bank of ChinaDated

BOC published a MoneyGram outbound fee schedule effective 2018-01-15. It documents a route and historical tiers but is not represented as a current 2026 quote.

Advantages

  • Bank-agent process and historical fee evidence
  • Potential cash-pickup alternative

Constraints

  • Current availability, price, FX and limits need fresh confirmation
  • Published non-Africa schedule ends at $9,999.99
  • No basis to extrapolate larger rows

Procedure

  1. Ask BOC whether MoneyGram outbound service is currently available at your branch/channel.
  2. Complete the same eligibility review and provide recipient/payout details.
  3. Obtain a current fee, FX, destination and per-transaction limit quote in writing.
  4. Compare final USD and pay only under the displayed terms.
  5. Save the reference number and supporting documents.
Cost treatment

The dated schedule’s $32 top tier ends at $9,999.99. Every $10,000-increment matrix cell is therefore blank; no current fee or higher tier is inferred.

8. Another licensed Chinese bank → USD wireUnpriced

SAFE’s foreign-individual rules are not BOC-specific. Another licensed bank may offer a better execution rate or document process, but no institution-wide current tariff was verified for ICBC, CCB or others.

Advantages

  • Potential competitive FX or service
  • Same licensed-bank evidence framework

Constraints

  • Do not reuse BOC’s fees as another bank’s fees
  • Channel availability and document practice vary

Procedure

  1. Ask the bank to classify your identity/source and pre-check the documents.
  2. Request its current personal cross-border USD tariff, execution rate, correspondent terms and receiving instructions.
  3. Compare total CNY debit and net USD against BOC/Wise.
  4. Execute and preserve the same evidence chain.
Cost treatment

All non-BOC institution-specific values remain blank; use the BOC matrix only as a benchmark, not a proxy.

9. Domestic-individual genuine current-account purchase/remittanceConditional

The $50,000 annual convenience purchase quota applies to domestic individuals. Genuine documented current-account purchases above it are possible; the quota is not a hard annual cap. A US passport holder should not assume this classification.

Advantages

  • Documented current-account needs are not artificially capped at $50,000
  • Electronic bank channels may be available

Constraints

  • Identity classification is decisive
  • Purpose must be genuine and documented
  • Annual-repeat modeling is not permission to split or disguise a transaction

Procedure

  1. Ask the bank which SAFE identity applies to the account; do not select domestic-individual treatment merely to obtain ID-only processing.
  2. For convenience-quota purchases, provide ID and truthful purpose information.
  3. Above the quota, provide amount-matched current-account purpose evidence; for outward FX-savings remittances, follow the applicable daily documentation rule.
  4. Keep purchase and payment purposes consistent and never borrow another person’s quota.
  5. Use the illustrative annual schedule only if each year’s request independently satisfies the rules.
Cost treatment

Illustrative $50k/year, one-wire-per-year model at constant rates: $400k over 8 years = $1,331.53. This is arithmetic, not a recommendation or evasion strategy.

10. Emigration property transferSpecial

SAFE provides a formal route for a qualifying emigrant to liquidate lawful property owned before obtaining the relevant emigration status and transfer it abroad. It is not a generic route for any savings.

Advantages

  • Designed for capital/property transfers
  • Can address amounts outside ordinary current-account income

Constraints

  • Strict applicant/property/tax eligibility
  • Full intended property amount must be addressed in the application
  • Staged-remittance rules and professional costs can apply

Procedure

  1. Confirm you meet the emigration/status and property-timing requirements in SAFE’s current service guide.
  2. Prepare the application, identity/settlement evidence, source and title documents, tax certificate/payment evidence and any agent documents.
  3. Apply to the SAFE office specified for the former household-registration location; disclose the full intended transfer.
  4. After approval, take the approval/registration documents to the designated bank for purchase/remittance.
  5. For staged transfers, follow the approved timing and present prior-remittance evidence at later stages.
  6. Retain approval, tax, FX and remittance records.
Cost treatment

If BOC executes the final wire, its published baseline applies. Matrix D models one approved remittance at or below CNY 200,000 and 50%/25%/25% above that threshold; taxes, legal/notary/agent and other professional costs remain blank.

11. Inherited PRC property → outward transferSpecial

A foreign citizen may use SAFE’s inheritance property-transfer process for lawfully inherited PRC assets after realization and required documentation.

Advantages

  • Explicit legal channel for inherited capital
  • Can cover financial assets as well as realized property

Constraints

  • Notarization, title, tax and deceased-person records
  • Different decedents and disputes can complicate review
  • Not a substitute for documenting ordinary savings

Procedure

  1. Confirm the assets form a lawful, undisputed inheritance and identify the correct SAFE jurisdiction.
  2. Prepare application and passport plus notarized inheritance evidence, decedent title documents, financial-asset records and tax evidence.
  3. Submit through the available SAFE service channel or with a properly documented agent.
  4. After approval, liquidate where required and have the bank execute purchase/remittance under the approval.
  5. Keep notarized, tax, sale/liquidation and final wire evidence.
Cost treatment

BOC baseline only if BOC is used. Legal, notary, liquidation, tax and professional fees —.

12. Foreign individual sells lawfully owned PRC home → remit after-tax proceedsSpecial

SAFE’s 2025 operating guide provides bank handling for a foreign individual’s registered sale of PRC residential property. Remittance cannot exceed the seller’s share of sale proceeds after included taxes/fees.

Advantages

  • Specific bank-based path for property capital
  • No artificial $50,000 convenience-quota ceiling stated for eligible proceeds

Constraints

  • Requires genuine registered sale and completed title transfer
  • Tax evidence must match the requested remittance
  • Property ownership and local eligibility facts matter

Procedure

  1. Verify original ownership and the seller’s foreign-individual status/share.
  2. Complete the genuine sale, registered contract and title transfer.
  3. Obtain the competent tax authority’s evidence for the transaction.
  4. Give the bank passport, registered sale/title documents and tax evidence; request remittance no greater than the after-tax seller share.
  5. Have the bank check consistency, execute purchase/remittance and complete balance-of-payments reporting.
  6. Keep the full ownership-to-remittance chain.
Cost treatment

BOC baseline if selected; property taxes, sale costs and adviser fees are excluded/blank.

13. Uncompleted foreign-buyer property purchase → refund/reconversionSpecial

The same SAFE guide covers returning unused RMB purchase funds when the property transaction fails. This is a reconversion/refund route, not a way to cycle unrelated CNY.

Advantages

  • Specific failed-purchase mechanism
  • Original path and interest treatment are addressed by the guide

Constraints

  • Original payment/conversion evidence required
  • Transaction-failure and regulatory records required
  • Cannot exceed the refundable amount

Procedure

  1. Collect the original payment and FX-conversion records.
  2. Obtain evidence that the purchase/registration failed or was lawfully cancelled, plus any required written explanation.
  3. Present passport and the regulator/escrow documents requested by the bank.
  4. Request reconversion and return along the original path or eligible same-name FX account as instructed.
  5. Document any permitted interest separately and retain all bank records.
Cost treatment

Only the documented refundable amount is eligible. Execution and receiving fees follow the chosen bank; current non-BOC values —.

14. Foreign direct-investment exit, equity sale or liquidation proceedsFact-specific

SAFE’s capital-account guide addresses repatriation of foreign-invested enterprise proceeds, including equity transfers and liquidation. This applies to genuine investment capital, not relabeling a personal bank balance.

Advantages

  • Formal channel for business capital
  • Bank and registration framework supports large documented transactions

Constraints

  • Corporate, registration, valuation and tax facts are decisive
  • May require transaction-control information and tax documents
  • Professional implementation is normally necessary

Procedure

  1. Map the entity, investor, registration and exact exit event.
  2. Obtain the relevant equity/liquidation documents and bank/SAFE registration-control information.
  3. Complete required corporate approvals and tax filing/payment evidence.
  4. Ask the handling bank to verify transaction authenticity, amount and registration data before purchase/remittance.
  5. Remit to the documented foreign investor and retain corporate, tax and bank records.
Cost treatment

Institution-specific costs and tax are blank. Do not apply the personal-income model to business proceeds.

15. Convert to USD cash → carry to the United StatesSmall only

China’s rules distinguish cash from account FX. Up to $5,000 equivalent needs no carrying permit; above $5,000 through $10,000 needs a bank permit. Above $10,000 is generally prohibited except specified special cases requiring SAFE permission.

Advantages

  • No international wire
  • BOC’s published carrying-permit fee is waived through 2026-12-31

Constraints

  • Loss/theft and travel risk
  • Not scalable beyond $10,000
  • China permit and US monetary-instrument declaration are separate

Procedure

  1. Establish lawful CNY source and complete the bank’s purchase/withdrawal review.
  2. For more than $5,000 through $10,000, obtain the bank carrying permit before departure; for a special case above $10,000, obtain SAFE permission rather than assuming it will be granted.
  3. Keep purchase, withdrawal and permit documents with the cash.
  4. At US entry, file FinCEN Form 105 when transporting more than $10,000 in currency/monetary instruments; exactly $10,000 is not ‘more than.’
  5. For a US bank cash deposit, respond to source questions and remember banks aggregate same-day cash over $10,000 for CTR purposes.
  6. Do not divide cash among people or days to evade China or US reporting.
Cost treatment

FX purchase spread can be modeled, but cash-withdrawal, travel, insurance, US deposit and security costs are blank. Amounts above $10,000 are marked generally infeasible, not priced as routine travel.

16. Chinese bank card → overseas ATM cashEmergency

SAFE caps domestic-card overseas ATM withdrawals at CNY 100,000 per person per year and CNY 10,000 per card per day. This produces cash, not a USD bank-account transfer, and borrowed-card evasion is prohibited.

Advantages

  • Immediate access to local currency abroad
  • Published BOC UnionPay fee cap per withdrawal

Constraints

  • Only the $10,000 scenario fits the annual cap at this FX rate
  • ATM owner surcharge and execution FX can add cost
  • Cash deposit/reporting and physical-security issues remain

Procedure

  1. Check your annual person-level withdrawal total and card-level daily total before traveling.
  2. Ask BOC which network/fee applies and request the ATM’s displayed surcharge and conversion terms before confirming.
  3. Withdraw within both limits using only your own card; do not borrow cards.
  4. Keep ATM and FX receipts; deposit cash transparently and answer bank source questions.
  5. Do not split deposits or use additional people/cards to bypass limits or reports.
Cost treatment

At $10k: 7 withdrawals at the CNY 10,000/card/day cap; current BOC UnionPay modeled fee $112.55; Visa/Mastercard formula effective 2026-11-11 $250.00. Other matrix rows exceed the annual cap. FX and ATM-owner costs —.

17. Hong Kong Payment Connect / offshore-CN H detourIncomplete

BOC describes mainland–Hong Kong Payment Connect for specified CNY/HKD scenarios. That is not evidence of an unrestricted CNY→USD transfer to the US, and routing through Hong Kong does not erase mainland source rules.

Advantages

  • May be useful for a genuine qualifying mainland–HK payment
  • Participating-bank channel

Constraints

  • Not a complete documented CNY→USD route
  • Southbound direction/residence and currency constraints
  • Second-leg FX/fees and US receipt unverified

Procedure

  1. Do not initiate a detour until both banks confirm the complete lawful path in writing.
  2. Verify your eligibility, direction, currency and purpose for the mainland–HK leg.
  3. Separately verify the HK account’s CNY/HKD→USD conversion and US remittance terms.
  4. Compare all legs and retain evidence; otherwise use the direct bank route.
Cost treatment

Every price cell remains blank because no complete official two-leg CNY→USD cost schedule was established.

18. PayPal China, standalone wallets, private swaps, crypto and false-trade workaroundsAvoid

No primary evidence was found for direct PayPal China or standalone Alipay/WeChat own-account CNY→USD repatriation. Wise’s use of Alipay as a funding rail is not proof of a standalone wallet service. Private swaps, borrowed quotas, false invoices and crypto P2P are not lawful substitutes.

Advantages

  • No verified advantage to recommend

Constraints

  • Unsupported product availability can strand funds
  • Private FX and evasion expose users to enforcement
  • PBOC prohibits virtual-currency-related business activities covered by its notice

Procedure

  1. Do not fund an unverified route.
  2. For a genuine wallet product, obtain operator terms explicitly supporting your passport, China funding, USD conversion and own US payout; otherwise leave it unused.
  3. Never borrow quotas, split to evade review, fabricate trade documents or use underground changers.
  4. Do not use crypto/stablecoins as a quota workaround; use a documented licensed route instead.
Cost treatment

No cost matrix is offered for prohibited or unproven workarounds. Unknown is not zero and technical feasibility is not legality.
