The conversion penalty, broken down

Eight separate cost layers, the officially published bank tariff, and how to measure the one number nobody publishes.

The eight cost layers

LayerCostWhat it is and when it bitesSource
1Chinese tax on the underlying incomeDue when the income arose or the asset was sold — not when you convert. Property transfer income is taxed at 20% of proceeds minus original value minus reasonable expenses. If this was never paid, it is the largest single cost and it blocks the property-transfer channels, which require a tax certificate.S11 S08
2The exchange-rate spreadThe bank quotes you a rate worse than the interbank rate and keeps the difference. Bank of China charges no separate conversion fee at its own branches, so this cost is invisible on any fee schedule. It scales with the amount, which makes it the dominant cost on a large transfer. No official figure is published, so it is left blank.S12 F06
3The bank's remittance tariff0.1% of the amount, minimum RMB 50, maximum RMB 260. Because it is capped, it becomes trivial as a percentage on a large wire — about 0.026% on a USD 140,000 equivalent transfer.S12
4Cable chargeRMB 150 flat for an international transfer. On a small transfer this dominates; on a large one it is noise.S12
5Intermediary and beneficiary bank chargesEither deducted from your principal in transit, or prepaid as a full-value-arrival surcharge of USD 15 (beneficiary bank accounts at BOC New York) or USD 25. Overseas bank charges are otherwise passed through at actual cost and are not published, so the amount cannot be stated in advance.S12
6US tax on the currency gainDisposing of RMB is a section 988 transaction producing ordinary gain or loss. The personal-transaction exclusion disappears entirely once the gain exceeds USD 200 — it is a cliff, not an allowance. A multi-year holding can easily produce a taxable gain.S19
7US reporting penalties if you skip a formNot a tax, and not proportional to the transfer. FBAR non-willful penalties are USD 16,536 per assessment; Form 8938 starts at USD 10,000 plus up to USD 50,000 more, with a 40% penalty on any related underpayment. These dwarf every bank fee on this page.S14 S16
8Document costsNotary, translation, consular legalisation, proof-of-funds certificates. The requirement is verified; the fee schedules are not, so no total is quoted.S08 S12 F11

How the layers add up — the shape of the cost, not invented numbers

No worked example on this page uses an assumed exchange rate or an assumed spread, because neither could be verified. What the official tariff does let us state exactly is the fixed and capped part.

Computed only from the published tariff: 0.1% capped at RMB 260, plus RMB 150 cable. Excludes the FX spread, intermediary charges and any tax — all of which are additional.

The bank tariff falls from 1.00% of a CNY 20,000 transfer to 0.041% of a CNY 1,000,000 transfer, because the fee is capped. This is why consolidating into fewer, larger wires reduces fees — but it does nothing about the spread, which scales linearly with the amount and is the cost worth negotiating.

How to measure the spread yourself

Because no official spread figure exists, the only honest number is one you observe. Do this at the moment you intend to transfer:

  1. Open your bank's published foreign-exchange quote page and note the cash-buying and cash-selling rates for USD, and the remittance (现汇) rates, timestamped.
  2. Note the same-day central parity rate published by CFETS under PBOC authority.
  3. The gap between the rate the bank will actually give you and the parity rate is your spread. Half the buying-selling gap is a reasonable proxy for the round-trip cost.
  4. Ask the branch for a firm quote on your actual amount. Large amounts are often quoted better than the board rate — this is the single highest-value negotiation available to you.
  5. Compare that all-in USD received figure against a second bank before committing.

Why this is left blank rather than estimated: figures around 0.24%–0.3% circulate widely, but only on secondary sites. Publishing one would violate this project's rule that every number comes from an official source. F06
