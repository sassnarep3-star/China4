What it really costs

Cost of converting and transferring CNY into USD, in $10,000 steps from $10,000 to $400,000. Figures are computed from two verified inputs — Bank of China's published USD rate and its published remittance tariff — plus statutory penalty ranges. Nothing is inferred.

Headline

On the documented bank route, moving $400,000 costs about $901 — 0.23%. Moving $10,000 costs about $53 (0.53%), because the fixed wire fee weighs more on a small amount. There is no tax in either figure. The alternative — an illegal changer — is priced at 30%–100% of the amount: $120,000–$400,000 at $400,000.

The inputs (verified)

Exchange rate

Bank of China quoted USD per 100 USD: buying 6.6648, selling 6.6929, conversion reference 6.7168. Quote timestamp 2026-11-09 05:30 Beijing time; page retrieved 2026-11-08. Because you are buying USD, the bank's selling rate applies.

Bank of China FX rates

Wire tariff

Cross-border telegraphic transfer: 1‰ of the RMB amount, minimum RMB 50, maximum RMB 260 per transfer, plus a RMB 150 cable charge outside Hong Kong/Macau/Taiwan. Schedule headed "effective 1 November 2014"; retrieved 2026-11-08.

Bank of China remittance tariff

Formula (re-check it by hand)

  • Mid rate = (6.6648 + 6.6929) ÷ 2 = 6.67885 CNY/USD.
  • FX spread cost = amount × (6.6929 − 6.67885) ÷ 6.6929 = amount × 0.002099 (0.210%).
  • RMB converted = amount × 6.6929.
  • Wire fee = clamp(0.001 × RMB converted, RMB 50, RMB 260) + RMB 150.
  • Total cost in USD = spread + (wire fee ÷ 6.6929).

The quota column counts calendar years needed if you rely on the US$50,000 annual quota alone: ⌈amount ÷ 50,000⌉. A couple filing and remitting separately doubles the annual capacity.

What these numbers deliberately exclude

  • US receiving-bank fee and correspondent/intermediary fees. Charged in transit by banks whose tariffs were not retrieved. Typically a fixed dollar amount; it does not scale with the transfer. [unverified]
  • Chinese bank charges for the conversion leg beyond the spread, and branch-level differences or promotions. [unverified]
  • Notarisation, translation, hukou-cancellation and legal fees for the property, emigration and inheritance routes. [unverified]
  • China individual income tax on untaxed income. This is a tax on the income, not on the transfer, and is outside a conversion-cost estimate. Rates: 3%–45% comprehensive, 20% on property transfer.
  • Any travel cost for cash carry. [unverified]
