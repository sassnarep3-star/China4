Cost estimator: $10,000 to $400,000

What it costs to convert CNY to USD and wire it to the US, in $10,000 steps. Every figure is computed from the published official inputs below — it is a model estimate, not a bank quote.

Read this first

The dominant cost is not the fee — it is the size of the amount relative to the rules. Because the individual annual foreign-exchange quota is US$50,000 per person per calendar year, moving more than that in one year requires either documentation of a legitimate, taxed current-account source (then SAFE Detailed Rules Art. 13 allows conversion with a tax certificate) or a SAFE-approved property transfer. The bank's own charges are small and capped: on the BOC published tariff the sending charge tops out at CNY 260 plus a CNY 150 cable fee — about $61.26 per wire at the rate used here, however large the transfer.

What is deliberately left blank: intermediary/correspondent bank charges and the US receiving bank's incoming-wire fee. No official published figure was found for either, so they are not filled in. Add them yourself from your two banks.

Why the quota route costs more above $50,000: it needs one wire per calendar year, so it pays the CNY 150 cable fee again each year and waits a year between tranches. Why the SAFE property-transfer route costs more: the rules require the money to leave in three stages — no more than half up front, no more than half of the remainder after one year, and the balance after two years (PBOC Interim Measures, Art. 6) — so it pays three sets of fees. Cash carry is capped by Arts. 5 and 7: above US$10,000 it is in principle not permitted.
