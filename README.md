# China4 — Moving Money Out of China (CNY → USD) for a US Citizen

Research-backed, source-verified guide and dashboard for legally converting and remitting
Chinese Yuan (CNY/RMB) to US Dollars (USD), aimed at a US citizen with funds in a Chinese
bank account.

## Live site

GitHub Pages: https://sassnarep3-star.github.io/China4/ (published from `main`, root)

## Contents

- `index.html` — landing page: executive summary + key figures + navigation
- `site/methods.html` — every legal (and prohibited) conversion/remittance channel with pros/cons
- `site/costs.html` — "conversion penalty" breakdown: what actually costs money, what is taxed, what is penalised
- `site/us-reporting.html` — US-side obligations (FBAR, Form 8938, Form 3520, FinCEN 105, IRC §988, FTC)
- `site/rules-china.html` — China-side FX rules (SAFE / PBOC / Customs / Tax)
- `site/flagged.html` — **Flagged for Review**: every claim that could not be verified from an
  official source line-by-line, with the nearest sources and how to confirm it
- `site/data/*.js` — single source of truth for all tables (one row = one sourced claim)
- `research/` — research notes and verification logs per topic
- `VERIFICATION.md` — methodology and evidence trail (what was checked, where, when)
- `scripts/check_links.py` — integrity check: internal links exist, every "verified" row has an
  official-domain source URL
- `COORDINATION.md` — shared multi-agent task board

## Verification policy

Every row marked **verified** links to an official source page (`.gov.cn`, `safe.gov.cn`,
`chinatax.gov.cn`, `irs.gov`, `fincen.gov`, `cbp.gov`, `govinfo.gov`, or the provider's own site
for commercial product facts) that was actually retrieved and read during research on 2026-11-07.
Anything not confirmed on an official page is left blank or moved to the Flagged for Review page.

Not tax, legal, or financial advice. Rules change — confirm at the official links before acting.
