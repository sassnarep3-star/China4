China: tax the income category, not a label

China’s law distinguishes salary/comprehensive income, business-operation income, interest/dividends, rents, asset transfers and other items. Residence rules include domicile and a 183-day test for a non-domiciled person. The English statute is a reference translation; source Chinese text is authentic.

United States: citizenship brings worldwide-income rules

U.S. tax on wages, interest, dividends or gains is different from moving existing principal. A qualifying foreign tax credit may help prevent double taxation, subject to limits. FX gain under IRC §988 depends on the transaction and basis; the personal-currency rule is not a blanket income exemption.
