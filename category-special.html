Foreign individual's mainland property-sale proceeds conditional

Remit sale proceeds with registered sale/title/tax documents through bank review. Migration-route assets owned before migration are a different procedure.

Eligibility: Foreign individual who lawfully purchased/owned mainland commercial housing; actual sale and tax clearance. Huifa [2012] 59 cancelled the obsolete SAFE purchase/remittance approval for this case.

Limits: Documented net sale proceeds and bank review; local tax/relief facts control. No universal USD 50,000 cap established for this route.

Pros

  • Source-matched capital/current treatment as applicable
  • Bank review replaces obsolete approval
  • Potential local IIT relief if genuinely qualified

Cons

  • Property/tax documents heavy
  • Local taxes and relief vary
  • US sale/gain/currency analysis required

Migration property transfer — pre-status assets conditional

Transfer lawful mainland assets owned before obtaining qualifying migration status through the competent former-hukou SAFE branch and bank.

Eligibility: Qualifying migration status and lawful undisputed assets owned before that status; not a general route for every US citizen.

Limits: Approved source/title/tax amount; competent-branch implementation controls. Historical staging text must be confirmed locally; administrative permission has no fee in the cited guide.

Pros

  • Designed for pre-migration assets
  • No administrative permission fee in cited guide
  • Direct own-overseas-account remittance

Cons

  • Status/ownership/tax evidence heavy
  • Branch approval and possible staging
  • Bank/professional costs separate

Inheritance property transfer — foreign citizen inheriting mainland estate conditional

Transfer inherited mainland property through the decedent's former-hukou SAFE branch with inheritance, title, identity and tax evidence.

Eligibility: Foreign citizen lawfully inheriting mainland estate; competent decedent former-hukou branch.

Limits: Approved inherited amount; multiple decedents may be combined at one relevant branch under the 2026 Q&A. Administrative permission has no fee in the cited guide.

Pros

  • Source-matched inheritance route
  • No administrative permission fee in cited guide
  • Delegation possible with proper documents

Cons

  • Inheritance/title/tax evidence heavy
  • Branch approval
  • US gift/estate/trust reporting may differ

Foreign-invested enterprise dividend to a foreign individual conditional

Distribute genuine FIE profits to a foreign individual with corporate approvals and withholding. From 2026-09-01 the former foreign-individual exemption is repealed and 20% PRC IIT applies.

Eligibility: Foreign individual shareholder of an FIE with distributable profits and proper corporate/tax/foreign-exchange documents.

Limits: Distributable profit and bank/registration review; treaty entitlement must be separately established. No transitional grandfathering is assumed.

Pros

  • Matches genuine equity income
  • Current 2026 rule verified
  • Potential treaty analysis if entitled

Cons

  • 20% PRC withholding under new rule
  • Corporate documents and tax filing
  • US dividend/FTC analysis required

Failed foreign-buyer property purchase — RMB refund reconversion conditional

SAFE 2025 guide covers returning unused RMB purchase funds when a foreign individual’s property transaction fails; refund follows the original path or eligible same-name FX account.

Eligibility: Foreign individual whose mainland property purchase failed/cancelled and whose RMB funds originated from a documented FX conversion/payment.

Limits: Refundable amount only; reasonable interest during domestic retention may be included; cannot cycle unrelated CNY.

Pros

  • Specific failed-purchase mechanism
  • Original-path and interest treatment addressed

Cons

  • Original payment/conversion evidence required
  • Transaction-failure and regulatory records required
  • Not a general savings route
