The Chinese rules that control

26 controlling rules, with the article, the Chinese text, and the errors people make reading them.

Start here: Are you a 境内个人 (domestic individual) or a 境外个人 (non-domestic individual)?

This decides whether the USD 50,000 annual purchase quota binds you at all. It is the single most commonly misstated point in English-language guides.

境内个人，是指中国公民和在中华人民共和国境内连续居住满1年的外国人，外国驻华外交人员和国际组织驻华代表除外。

Translation: A domestic individual means a Chinese citizen, or a foreigner who has resided in the PRC continuously for a full one year, excluding foreign diplomatic personnel in China and representatives of international organisations in China.

A US citizen who has lived in China continuously for one year IS a domestic individual and IS subject to the USD 50,000 annual purchase quota. A US citizen who has not is a non-domestic individual and uses Article 13 instead. S02

R01The annual USD 50,000 total — what it actually covers
Detailed Rules Art. 2
Annual total management applies to SETTLEMENT (结汇, foreign currency to RMB) by ALL individuals, and to PURCHASE (购汇, RMB to foreign currency) by DOMESTIC individuals only. Each annual total is USD 50,000 equivalent per person per year. SAFE may adjust it.
Chinese text
对个人结汇和境内个人购汇实行年度总额管理。年度总额分别为每人每年等值5万美元。
Common error: Guides routinely say 'you can only convert USD 50,000 a year' without noting that the purchase-side limit is written only for 境内个人.

R03Non-domestic individual converting lawful RMB income
Detailed Rules Art. 13(1)
Lawful current-account RMB income earned in China is converted to FX on presentation of valid personal ID plus supporting documents evidencing the transaction amount, INCLUDING tax payment vouchers.

在境内取得的经常项目合法人民币收入，凭本人有效身份证件和有交易额的相关证明材料（含税务凭证）办理购汇。

R05Domestic individual remitting FX abroad from a savings account
Detailed Rules Art. 14
From an FX savings account: same-day cumulative up to USD 50,000 equivalent on valid ID alone; above that, current-account authenticity vouchers showing the transaction amount. Hand-held FX banknotes: same-day cumulative up to USD 10,000 equivalent on ID alone; above that, authenticity vouchers plus a Customs-stamped inbound declaration or the original bank's FX withdrawal slip.

Common error: This USD 50,000 is PER DAY for remitting FX you already hold. It is a different number from the PER YEAR purchase quota in Art. 2, even though both say 5万美元.

R08Bank authenticity review is mandatory
Detailed Rules Art. 6; Order 532 Art. 12
Banks must verify the authenticity of individual FX business and must not fabricate or alter transactions. Current-account FX receipts and payments must have a genuine and lawful transaction basis.
Chinese text
各外汇指定银行……应按照本细则规定对个人外汇业务进行真实性审核，不得伪造、变造交易。
—S01 S02
R09Current-account international payments are not restricted
Order 532 Art. 5
The State does not restrict current-account international payments and transfers. The controls are on verification and on capital account, not a blanket prohibition on sending money out.
Chinese text
国家对经常性国际支付和转移不予限制。

R10Withdrawing FX banknotes from a Chinese bank
Detailed Rules Art. 30
Same-day cumulative withdrawals up to USD 10,000 equivalent are handled directly at the bank. Above that you must pre-file with the local SAFE bureau, providing ID and proof of the purpose, and the bank acts on the SAFE-stamped filing form.

R12Transfers between your own accounts and to close family
Detailed Rules Art. 28
Between your own accounts: valid ID. Between you and a direct family member: both IDs plus proof of the direct family relationship. Between a domestic and a non-domestic individual: managed as a CROSS-BORDER transaction.
Chinese text
（三）境内个人和境外个人账户间的资金划转按跨境交易进行管理。
In practice: Parking money in a Chinese friend's or relative's account to use their quota is not a domestic transfer if one of you is a non-domestic individual — it is treated as cross-border and pulls in the cross-border rules.
—S01
R13Carrying foreign-currency banknotes OUT of China
Huifa [2003] No. 102, covering notice item 2
Up to USD 5,000 equivalent: no permit, Customs releases. Above USD 5,000 up to and including USD 10,000: a Carrying Permit issued by a designated FX bank, stamped, is required. In principle no more than USD 10,000 equivalent may be carried out; above that a SAFE permit is available only for listed special cases (large delegations, long scientific expeditions, government leaders, travel to war zones or countries with strict FX controls, other special situations).

R15Carrying payment instruments is not controlled by Customs
Huifa [2003] No. 102, covering notice item 3
Because foreign-currency payment instruments and securities are brought into the banking management system, Customs no longer administers their carriage in and out of the country. This is why a bank draft is not subject to the banknote permit tiers.
Chinese text
对出入境人员携带上述凭证和证券出入境，海关不再予以管理。

R16Outbound property transfer: the two lawful channels
PBOC [2004] No. 16 Art. 2; Detailed Rules Art. 20
Only two: emigration property transfer by a person who emigrated from mainland China, and inheritance transfer by a foreign citizen who lawfully inherited a Chinese estate. Detailed Rules Art. 20 expressly cross-refers to these Interim Measures.
Chinese text
境内个人将其取得合法移民身份前境内财产对外转移以及外国公民依法继承境内遗产的对外转移，按《个人财产对外转移售付汇管理暂行办法》等有关规定办理。
—S01 S08

R20When SAFE will refuse a property-transfer application
PBOC [2004] No. 16 Art. 12
SAFE will not accept the application where the property is subject to judicial or administrative restriction, is involved in pending criminal or civil proceedings, or where the lawful source of the property cannot be demonstrated.—S08
R21Cross-border RMB settlement of individual current-account income
Yinfa [2020] No. 330 item 13
Banks are supported in handling individuals' current-account cross-border RMB settlement, expressly facilitating cross-border receipts and payments for salary and other lawful income. This moves RMB, not FX, so no onshore conversion occurs.—S07
R22Quota splitting and the watch list
Huifa [2015] No. 49 item 2
Splitting to evade quota or authenticity management is prohibited. Lending your quota draws a risk-warning letter first and watch-listing on repetition; borrowing another's quota draws immediate watch-listing. The watch-list period is the year of listing plus the following two consecutive years, during which every transaction requires documentary proof at the counter.3 yearsS03
R23Crypto is not a lawful channel
Yinfa [2021] No. 237 items 1(2), 1(3), 1(4), 4(9)
Virtual-currency-related business activity is an illegal financial activity. Fiat-to-crypto exchange is strictly prohibited; overseas exchanges serving mainland residents online are likewise illegal. Banks and payment institutions may not provide accounts, transfers or settlement. Related civil acts contrary to public order are void and losses fall on the participant.—S06
R24Chinese tax on the underlying income
IIT Law Art. 1, 3, 6
A tax resident is a person domiciled in China, or not domiciled but resident for 183 days or more in a tax year. Comprehensive income is taxed at 3%-45%; business income at 5%-35%; interest, dividends, property lease, property transfer and incidental income at 20%. Property-transfer income is proceeds minus original value minus reasonable expenses.
In practice: This is the tax that actually matters on a property sale. It is due on the GAIN when the asset is sold, not on the later act of converting or remitting the proceeds.

R25Tax clearance before cancelling Chinese household registration
IIT Law Art. 10(5) and Art. 13
A taxpayer who emigrates and cancels Chinese household registration must settle the tax payable before the cancellation.
In practice: The hukou-cancellation certificate required for an emigration transfer (R18) therefore presupposes tax clearance. This is why the tax step cannot be skipped.
