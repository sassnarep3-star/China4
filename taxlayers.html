Read this before any cost table. No provision imposing a tax on the act of exchanging CNY for USD was found in the texts reviewed here (Individual Income Tax Law Art. 2 lists nine taxable income categories and none is a currency exchange; the FX regulations govern permission and documentation, not a conversion levy). The money that hurts is (1) PRC income tax that was never paid on the income behind the balance, (2) PRC late-payment surcharge and fines on that unpaid tax, (3) US income tax on the same income, and (4) US information-return penalties. A limited negative finding is not a legal opinion — see the review limits below. IIT Law Art. 2 FX Regulations

What the bank will actually demand

The annual USD 50,000 convenience total covers settlement by all individuals and purchase by domestic individuals. A foreign individual is not inside that purchase quota; the route is the documented current-account purchase, which requires amount-linked documents including tax vouchers.

If the PRC tax was never paid: surcharge and fine

Unpaid tax carries a daily surcharge of 0.05% of the overdue tax from the day it became overdue. A failure to file can also attract a fine of 50% to five times the unpaid tax where the conduct is treated as evasion or non-filing; administrative penalties are barred if the conduct was not discovered within five years. These are statutory maxima and ranges, not predictions. Tax Collection and Administration Law Arts. 32, 62–64, 86

How to read that table. Both columns assume the entire balance is untaxed income of a single character, which is almost never true; salary is taxed progressively and the first CNY 60,000 is deducted, and property tax is charged on the gain, not the price. The columns exist to show the size of the exposure and why establishing that tax was already paid is worth more than any bank-fee optimisation on this site.

The order that minimises total cost

Sequence matters more than route choice: paying PRC tax late adds 0.05% a day, and an information-return penalty can exceed every bank fee on this site combined.

Sequential order of operations

1Fix the identity firstThe bank will treat you as a domestic or foreign individual. The two follow different purchase rules, and the foreign-individual route is the one that requires tax vouchers. Get the classification in writing before pricing anything.Art. 2, Art. 13

What this page does not establish

  • The absence of a PRC conversion tax is a limited negative finding across the IIT Law, the FX Regulations and the individual FX rules reviewed here. It is not a legal opinion and it does not cover local practice, provincial guidance or rules outside those texts. IIT Law FX Regulations
  • No personal tax rate, residency status, deduction or treaty eligibility is assumed. Whether you are a PRC resident individual (domicile or 183 days) changes the answer. Art. 1
  • Current bank and provider prices are not verified anywhere on this site; the tables use a historical FX snapshot to size amounts only. See Flagged for Review.
  • US state tax, exit tax, gift and estate consequences and PRC social insurance are outside this page.
  • The 20% and 45% columns are hypothetical arithmetic on statutory rates, not an estimate of your liability.
