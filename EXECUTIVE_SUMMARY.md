# Executive summary — lawful CNY-to-USD options for a US citizen

**Research date:** 2026-11-08. **Scope:** federal US tax/reporting plus mainland China foreign-exchange, tax and operator-published terms. This is research, not a personalized tax opinion or an executable quote.

## Most important answer first

1. **Start with the actual source of the CNY, not a generic “$50,000 wire.”** For a foreign individual with documented lawful China salary or other current-account RMB income, the usual lawful path is bank conversion/remittance with identity, transaction-amount source evidence and tax evidence. The USD 50,000 figure is a facilitation quota for settlement and domestic-individual purchases, not a universal annual outbound cap. [SAFE amended rules](https://www.safe.gov.cn/tianjin/2024/0430/2464.html); [SAFE FAQ](https://www.safe.gov.cn/xiamen/file/file/20211022/f4794075e84e40dd9394be523a52731b.pdf); [ICBC purchase guide](https://www.icbc.com.cn/page/721852492064522245.html).
2. **Compare same-time, all-in net-USD quotes.** Ask for the executed CNY/USD rate, sending fee, cable fee, fee allocation (SHA/OUR), correspondent/receiving charges, validity time and expected credited amount. The legacy site’s 1%–3% spread and USD 15–50 wire estimates were unsupported and are removed. [BOC current tariff](https://pic.bankofchina.com/bocappd/pricelist/202511/P020251110646838136627.pdf); [Wise pricing explanation](https://wise.com/us/pricing/send-money?source=CNY&target=USD&payInMethod=BANK_TRANSFER&payOutMethod=BANK_TRANSFER&sourceAmount=66538).
3. **Do not assume “already taxed in China” means “no US tax.”** US citizens generally report worldwide income. Moving previously reported principal is not a second income item, but the underlying salary/interest/dividend/gain and any CNY disposition under IRC §988 still need analysis. The personal-currency gain exception is narrow and fails when the gain on the transaction exceeds USD 200. [IRS worldwide income FAQ](https://www.irs.gov/individuals/international-taxpayers/frequently-asked-questions-about-international-individual-tax-matters); [IRC §988](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USCODE-2024-title26/pdf/USCODE-2024-title26-subtitleA-chap1-subchapN-partIII-subpartJ-sec988.pdf).
4. **Reporting is not a transfer tax.** FBAR is triggered by aggregate foreign-account value over USD 10,000 at any time in the calendar year, not by a USD 10,000 transfer. Form 8938 has different residence/filing-status thresholds. Form 3520 concerns specified foreign gifts/trusts, not an ordinary transfer of your own money. [FinCEN FBAR](https://www.fincen.gov/report-foreign-bank-and-financial-accounts); [IRS Form 8938 instructions](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i8938); [IRS Form 3520 instructions](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i3520).
5. **Never use underground matchers, borrowed quotas, fake invoices or a mainland CNY-to-crypto-to-USD exchange workaround.** These are excluded, not “cheap alternatives.” Current PBOC 2026 No. 42 repeals the 2021 notice and treats mainland virtual-currency-related business as illegal financial activity. [SAFE FX Regulations](https://www.chinamoney.com.cn/english/rarfmrrudrgl/20080805/813.html); [PBOC 2026 No. 42](https://www.pbc.gov.cn/tiaofasi/144941/3581332/2026020619591971323/index.html).

## What is verified about costs

- **BOC current personal tariff, effective 2025-11-11:** cross-border TT/draft commission is **0.1%**, minimum **CNY 50**, maximum **CNY 260** per transaction; TT cable is **CNY 150** outside HK/Macao/TW or **CNY 80** to those regions; draft postage is actual. Overseas bank charges are actual. A conditional USD full-arrival service is **USD 15/25** depending on routing, not a universal correspondent-fee cap. [BOC tariff](https://pic.bankofchina.com/bocappd/pricelist/202511/P020251110646838136627.pdf).
- **SkyRemit:** an official notice publishes **CNY 79** per transaction and **CNY 300,000** per transfer; current terms say the final billed amount controls. Treat CNY 79 as a reference component, not a guaranteed quote. [SkyRemit notice](https://www.skyremit.cn/news/blog-detail-103.html); [terms](https://remit.skyee360.com/client/account/terms-privacy).
- **Wise:** eligibility and funding caps are published, but no static CNY-to-USD fee quote was verified. Do not import another corridor’s percentage. [Wise CNY guide](https://wise.com/help/articles/2955298/guide-to-cny-transfers).
- **Western Union:** official China page verifies partner-bank outbound service and cash-pickup instructions, not a personalized US-bank deposit quote, fee or limit. [WU China](https://www.westernunion.com/cn/en/send-money.html).
- **ATM/cash:** BOC international-debit overseas ATM fee is currently 0.5%, min USD 3/max USD 5; ordinary debit is CNY 12 + 1%, max CNY 112. A announced change takes effect **2026-11-11**, after the research date. BOC carrying-permit fee is CNY 10 but waived through 2026-12-31. [BOC tariff](https://pic.bankofchina.com/bocappd/pricelist/202511/P020251110646838136627.pdf); [future ATM notice](https://www.boc.cn/pbservice/bi2/202608/t20260811_25684907.html).
- **No all-in USD total is published.** The cost tables contain 40 rows for each of 32 methods, from USD 10,000 through USD 400,000 (1,280 rows). Unknown FX spread, routing, tax, professional costs, all-in cost and net USD are blank and flagged. A Federal Reserve historical observation is reference data only, not a customer quote. [Fed H.10](https://www.federalreserve.gov/releases/h10/hist/dat00_ch.htm).

## Tax and reporting distinctions that change the answer

- **China:** residence is domicile/183-day based, not citizenship-based. Comprehensive income rates are 3%–45%; business 5%–35%; specified passive/property categories ordinarily 20%. Savings-deposit interest accrued from 2008-10-09 is temporarily exempt from PRC IIT, not US tax. [IIT law](https://www.chinatax.gov.cn/n810219/n810744/n3752930/n3752974/c3970366/content.html); [interest notice](https://fgk.chinatax.gov.cn/zcfgk/c102416/c5203286/content.html).
- **US relief:** compare Form 1116 FTC with Form 2555 FEIE where eligible; do not claim both on the same income. The 2026 FEIE maximum is **USD 132,900**, earned-income only, with tax-home and residence/presence tests. Treaty benefits require actual eligibility. [IRS FTC](https://www.irs.gov/individuals/international-taxpayers/foreign-tax-credit); [Form 2555 instructions](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i2555); [China treaty](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-trty/china.pdf).
- **FBAR penalties:** current eCFR adjusted maxima are **USD 16,536** non-willful and **USD 165,353** willful for assessments on/after 2025-01-17; the willful statute also has a 50%-of-balance alternative. *Bittner* holds non-willful penalties accrue per report, not per account. These are not automatic fees. [eCFR](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/subtitle-B/chapter-X/part-1010/subpart-H/section-1010.821); [Bittner](https://www.supremecourt.gov/opinions/22pdf/21-1195_h3ci.pdf).
- **Form 8938:** initial USD 10,000 failure penalty; continuing USD 10,000 per 30 days after the IRS notice/90-day period, additional maximum USD 50,000. [Instructions](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i8938).
- **Form 3520:** foreign individual/estate gifts/bequests over USD 100,000 aggregate; 2026 foreign entity threshold USD 20,573; foreign-gift late penalty 5%/month up to 25%. Trust rules differ. [Instructions](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i3520); [IRS gifts page](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/gifts-from-foreign-person).

## Bottom line

For most large, lawful balances, the lowest-risk starting point is a **source-matched bank conversion/remittance** or an **eligible own-account salary service**, chosen only after comparing same-time all-in quotes and confirming tax/reporting. The legitimate tax cost is minimized by correctly documenting the underlying income/gain, claiming only eligible FTC/FEIE/treaty relief, preserving CNY basis records and filing required reports—not by evading FX controls or assuming a transfer is tax-free.
