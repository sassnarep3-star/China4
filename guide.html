Decision guide

Four questions that determine your route, then how to minimise the cost.

Question 1 — Which rule set applies to you?

Under Order 532 Art. 52(2) you are a domestic individual (境内个人) if you are a Chinese citizen or a foreigner who has resided in China continuously for one full year. Otherwise you are a non-domestic individual (境外个人).

境内个人，是指中国公民和在中华人民共和国境内连续居住满1年的外国人，外国驻华外交人员和国际组织驻华代表除外。

Question 3 — Have you met your US reporting duties?

Do this before moving money, not after. A large inbound wire can prompt questions, and the penalties for unfiled forms are unrelated to whether you owe any tax.

Question 4 — How do you minimise the cost?

  1. Settle any Chinese tax first. It is the largest layer and it gates the capital-account channels. S11 S08
  2. Get a firm quote on your actual amount. The spread is the dominant cost and the board rate is often not the best available rate for a large conversion. See the conversion cost page. S12 F06
  3. Consolidate into fewer, larger wires. The wire fee is capped at RMB 260, so its percentage falls sharply with size. Splitting a transfer multiplies the RMB 150 cable charge for no benefit. S12
  4. Decide deliberately who pays intermediary charges. The full-value-arrival surcharge is USD 15 or USD 25; without it, correspondents deduct from your principal at unpublished rates. S12
  5. Use UnionPay, not Mastercard or VISA, for overseas cash after 2026-11-11. UnionPay stays at RMB 12 + 1% capped at RMB 112; the other networks move to 2.5% capped at USD 40. S25
  6. Never buy speed with an illegal channel. Underground banks, crypto and quota splitting carry fines up to the entire amount in serious cases, plus criminal exposure. S02 S06 S03

Red flags in advice you may be given

What you might hearWhy it is wrongSource
“Just use five relatives' quotas.”Quota splitting. Both lender and borrower are targeted; the watch-list period is the year of listing plus two more years.S03
“Use USDT, it's instant and nobody checks.”Fiat-to-crypto exchange is an illegal financial activity in China; related civil acts can be void, so you may have no recourse if the counterparty disappears.S06
“Pay RMB here, receive USD there.”This is mirror settlement (对敲) — disguised FX trading under Art. 45, up to 30% and in serious cases up to 100% of the amount, plus criminal exposure.S02
“The fine can be 500%.”Not in the statute. The ceiling is 100% of the amount. Anyone quoting 300% or 500% has not read Order 532.S02
“You can only ever move USD 50,000 a year.”Only for domestic individuals purchasing FX, and even then Art. 12 allows more with documents. Inheritance and emigration transfers have no annual quota.S01 S08
“There's no US tax when you convert.”Disposing of RMB is a section 988 transaction. The USD 200 personal exclusion vanishes entirely above USD 200 of gain.S19
