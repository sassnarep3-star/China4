Wise — eligible China salary to own overseas account operator verified

Wise publishes a CNY salary-remittance service for non-mainland-Chinese individuals with current/prior China work and qualifying tax records.

Eligibility: Non-mainland-Chinese individual; worked in China; tax records for the relevant period; personal transfer to own overseas account/Wise account; own-name funding.

Limits: Overall limit generally documented salary net of China tax; bank-app funding CNY 300,000/transfer and Alipay CNY 50,000/transfer in the fetched guide. Current document review controls.

Pros

  • Online own-account flow
  • Published eligibility and funding caps
  • Mid-market pricing claim with transparent quote at transaction time

Cons

  • Narrower than law
  • Tax-record example is stale
  • No static CNY-to-USD fee quote verified

SkyRemit — after-tax China income to own overseas account operator verified

SkyRemit publishes after-tax mainland income remittance to the customer's own overseas account, with compliance review and a CNY 300,000 per-transaction notice.

Eligibility: Customer with lawful after-tax mainland income; own overseas account; special consular/diplomatic evidence exception described in the notice.

Limits: Published CNY 300,000 per transaction; up to ten bank funding transfers may be consolidated in the notice; current terms and final billed amount control.

Pros

  • Own-account salary flow
  • Published CNY 79 reference fee
  • Published per-transaction cap

Cons

  • Undated fee notice; terms allow changes
  • Indicative rate until confirmation
  • No guaranteed completion time; correspondent delays possible

Western Union via China partner banks operator verified

Outbound service through partner banks; the fetched China page describes online/agent sending and overseas cash pickup, not a verified direct-to-US-bank quote.

Eligibility: Partner-bank customer meeting WU/bank identity, destination and passport/risk rules; BOC online is mobile-only in the fetched page.

Limits: Operator/agent/destination limits and fees not verified for a US passport or US bank deposit. Do not assume a USD 50,000 operator cap.

Pros

  • Published partner-bank channel
  • Cash-pickup option where available
  • Useful for smaller urgent needs after quote

Cons

  • Direct US-bank delivery unverified
  • Fees/FX/limits unknown
  • Cash pickup adds handling and declaration issues

Alipay international remittance — official quota product unverified

Alipay's official page describes partner-financial-institution remittance for verified real-name users and a domestic-individual USD 50,000 purchase quota check.

Eligibility: Page describes domestic-individual quota treatment; foreign-passport eligibility, current fees and US-bank delivery are not verified.

Limits: Official page cites USD 50,000 annual purchase quota for domestic individuals; current product limits/fees unverified. Do not use promotional flash-remit claims.

Pros
