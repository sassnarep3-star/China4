Genuine individual trade / cross-border e-commerce settlement conditional

Use a personal foreign-exchange settlement account or compliant e-commerce/market-procurement settlement for real trade, with contracts and transport/transaction evidence.

Eligibility: Registered individual trade operator/individual business or qualifying cross-border e-commerce/market-procurement activity; not personal savings relabeled as trade.

Limits: Actual transaction amount and institutional-style account rules; documented e-commerce/market-procurement settlement may not use the personal facilitation quota.

Pros

  • Lawful business-source route
  • Documented trade can avoid personal quota treatment
  • Matches real commercial activity

Cons

  • Registration and business records required
  • Business tax and possibly US entity reporting
  • Fake invoices are illegal

Lawful current-account RMB remittance, then offshore conversion conditional

Remit RMB for a genuine current-account purpose under bank due diligence, then convert offshore if the receiving bank accepts RMB. This is not blanket capital-account permission.

Eligibility: Individual with lawful current-account RMB income/purpose accepted by the domestic bank; receiving bank must accept RMB and offer conversion.

Limits: Bank KYC/KYB/due diligence and actual current-account purpose; no verified personal capital-account outbound quota. Offshore conversion is separate.

Pros

  • Avoids premature onshore conversion
  • PBOC current-account policy verified
  • May allow offshore rate comparison

Cons

  • Receiving-bank acceptance unverified
  • Two-step FX/routing costs
  • Not a quota-evasion or capital-account workaround
