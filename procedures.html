Step-by-step procedures

Procedures are source-matched; they do not authorize quota evasion or false documents.

Bank telegraphic transfer — documented China salary verified route

Convert lawful after-tax China salary with source/tax evidence and remit by SWIFT to your own US account. BOC tariff is the published cost example.

Eligibility: Foreign individual with lawful mainland current-account salary; bank classification and document review required. Not a universal USD 50,000 cap.

Limits: Amount supported by documented salary net of China tax and bank review; no fixed legal cap established in the cited rules. Operator/channel limits may apply.

Documents / inputs

  • Passport/ID and bank classification
  • Employment contract
  • Income details/payslips
  • China tax records or evidence no PRC tax is due
  • US recipient name, account, SWIFT/BIC and address

Exact steps

  1. Ask the bank to confirm foreign-individual classification and the salary-purchase checklist.
  2. Collect contract, income details and tax records for the exact amount; reconcile salary already reported to the IRS.
  3. Obtain a same-time CNY/USD quote and written fee allocation (SHA/OUR), including cable and expected correspondent/receiving charges.
  4. Complete the purchase/remittance application truthfully; do not borrow quotas or relabel funds.
  5. Authorize conversion and SWIFT transfer to your own US account; retain the memo, receipt and SWIFT confirmation.
  6. Reconcile the credited USD amount, keep FX/basis records, and update FBAR/Form 8938 reporting if triggered.

Eligibility: Foreign individual with lawful mainland current-account RMB income; the bank must accept the actual source category and documents.

Limits: Documented transaction amount; no universal USD 50,000 purchase quota for foreign individuals. Capital-account proceeds need a different route.

Exact steps

  3. Resolve China tax and US worldwide-income reporting before conversion.
  4. Obtain a same-time quote and fee allocation.
  5. Submit truthful source documents and remit to your own US account.
  6. Retain receipts and reconcile currency gain/loss and account reporting.

Bank telegraphic transfer — unused RMB from an earlier exchange verified route

Reconvert unused RMB using the original exchange memo within 24 months, or qualifying foreign-card RMB withdrawal evidence within six months at ICBC.

Eligibility: Foreign individual with unused RMB originally obtained by exchange or qualifying foreign-card withdrawal; small ID-only reconversion is limited to USD 500/day or USD 1,000 at qualifying departure ports.

Limits: Original memo amount and 24-month validity; ICBC describes six-month foreign-card withdrawal evidence. Small daily ID-only bands are not a large-transfer route.

Documents / inputs

  • ID
  • Original exchange memo or qualifying card withdrawal evidence
  • Unused RMB amount reconciliation
  • US recipient details

Exact steps

  1. Locate the original memo/withdrawal evidence and check the date and unused amount.
  2. Confirm the bank accepts the evidence and the amount does not exceed the original conversion.
  3. Obtain quote and fee allocation.
  4. Complete reconversion and remittance application.
  5. Send to your own US account and retain the reversal receipt.
  6. Analyze any currency gain/loss and reporting.

Bank telegraphic transfer — person classified as a domestic individual conditional

If the bank classifies the customer as a domestic individual, use the facilitation quota or documented genuine current-account demand; do not assume US citizenship controls the classification.

Eligibility: Only after bank classification. Domestic individuals have USD 50,000 annual purchase facilitation; above it, genuine current-account transaction evidence is required.

Limits: USD 50,000 annual purchase facilitation; foreign-currency account remittance USD 50,000/day ID-only, above with current-account evidence; cash remittance USD 10,000/day ID-only. Unopened capital-account purposes are not authorized by the quota.

Documents / inputs

  • ID and bank classification
  • Purchase application
  • Genuine current-account transaction evidence if above quota
  • US recipient details

Exact steps

  1. Obtain the bank's written classification and available facilitation/quota status.
  2. Identify a genuine current-account purpose and collect transaction evidence if needed.
  3. Do not split or borrow quotas to evade controls.
  4. Obtain quote and fee allocation.
  5. Complete purchase/remittance and retain receipts.
  6. Reconcile US tax and account reporting.

Bank demand draft verified route

Buy a USD demand draft with lawful CNY source evidence and deliver/deposit it abroad. Slower and acceptance-dependent than SWIFT.

Eligibility: Same lawful source/conversion rules as the underlying purchase; receiving bank must accept the draft.

Limits: Source-supported amount; draft acceptance, collection time and receiving-bank fees are not verified.

Documents / inputs

  • ID and lawful source evidence
  • Payee name/address
  • Receiving-bank draft acceptance instructions

Exact steps

  1. Confirm the US bank accepts a BOC USD demand draft and ask about collection fees/hold time.
  2. Complete the lawful CNY purchase with source documents.
  3. Complete the draft application and pay published commission plus actual postage.
  4. Securely deliver the draft and deposit it according to the receiving bank's instructions.
  5. Retain draft/receipt and reconcile collection, fees and reporting.

Documents / inputs

Exact steps

Limits: Approved source/title/tax amount; competent-branch implementation controls. Historical staging text must be confirmed locally; administrative permission has no fee in the cited guide.

Documents / inputs

  • Application form
  • ID and migration/settlement status evidence

  • Asset source/title documents
  • Tax clearance/proof
  • 委托代理协议 and agent ID if delegated

Exact steps

  1. Identify the competent former-hukou SAFE branch and obtain its current checklist.

  3. Collect source/title and tax documents for each asset.

  5. After approval, confirm any staging and complete bank purchase/remittance to your own overseas account.

Overseas ATM withdrawal with a mainland card verified route

Withdraw local cash abroad using a mainland card, subject to SAFE's per-person annual and per-card daily caps. Unsuitable for large balances.

Eligibility: Cardholder using own mainland card abroad; no borrowing/lending cards. BOC international-debit tariff is the published example.

Limits: CNY 100,000 equivalent per person per calendar year across all domestic cards; CNY 10,000 equivalent per card per day. Exceeding annual cap suspends overseas withdrawals for current and following year.

Documents / inputs

  • Own card and PIN
  • Travel/identity documents
  • Cash declaration records if crossing borders with thresholds

Exact steps

  1. Confirm card network, overseas withdrawal enablement and remaining annual/daily cap.
  2. Check the BOC tariff effective on the withdrawal date and the ATM surcharge screen.
  3. Withdraw only within CNY 10,000/card/day and CNY 100,000/person/year.
  4. Keep receipts and track all cards under your name.
  5. If carrying cash across the US border, aggregate family/group amounts and file FinCEN 105 when >USD 10,000.
  6. Do not use repeated withdrawals to evade caps.
