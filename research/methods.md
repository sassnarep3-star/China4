# Methods and costs

The methods page compares documented income conversion, eligible quota use, card withdrawals, cash carriage, asset transfers and provider-specific services. See its links to SAFE, PBOC and provider materials. Source of funds matters: salary, inheritance, property proceeds and prior currency exchanges can follow different rules. Actual bank spreads, charges, restrictions and account acceptance must be confirmed with the provider. Illegal FX dealing and quota evasion are not recommended. A source-of-funds tax payment requirement is not proof of a universal conversion tax.
