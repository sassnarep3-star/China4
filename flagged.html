🚩 B. Irregularities and conflicts

Irregularity Detail Severity Source(s) in conflict
Does the US$50,000 annual quota apply to foreigners? SAFE’s Detailed Rules set a US$50,000 annual settlement/purchase quota for individuals. Bank pages (HSBC, Standard Chartered) distinguish domestic individuals (settlement & purchase quota US$50,000) from non-domestic individuals (settlement quota US$50,000; purchase “no quota”). Some expat guides say foreigners can remit unlimited after-tax salary with tax records. The precise boundary for a US citizen who is not employed in China is unclear. High SAFE Detailed Rules vs. HSBC/Standard Chartered China pages vs. expat guides (secondary)
“Ants moving” (borrowing family/friends’ quotas) Widely discussed in practice; SAFE treats quota borrowing as evasion if structured to evade controls (fines 30%–300%). Not explicitly named in the regulation text we reviewed. Medium SAFE Regulations Art. 38/39 (evasion) vs. community practice
