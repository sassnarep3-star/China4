Verification policy

1

Primary evidence

Regulator, statute, court/regulatory text, issuing bank or operator. Secondary travel/expat estimates are not used as facts.

2

Date and scope

Old tariffs remain only when labeled historical; future changes show their effective date; product availability is not inferred from branding.

3

Blank means unknown

No unconfirmed fee, tax, limit or route is backfilled. Conditional scenarios use disclosed inputs and reproducible formulas.

Research, not individualized legal, tax, or investment advice. Eligibility follows the source and character of funds, identity used at the bank, tax residence, and transaction purpose. Obtain a written all-in bank/provider quote and qualified cross-border tax review before execution. “—” means unknown or unavailable, never zero.
