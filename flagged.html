The published rate board is not an executable quoteBank of China states on the board itself that it is for reference and that the rate actually applied at online banking, mobile banking, smart counters or the branch counter governs. The estimator therefore uses it as an input, not as a promise.BOC FX rate board disclaimer
Anomaly: quoted selling rate below the PBOC central parity rateOn the snapshot date the bank’s published USD selling rate (6.6929) sat below the PBOC/CFETS central parity rate (6.7168), the opposite of the usual relationship. The estimator therefore uses the internally consistent half-spread measure rather than a comparison with parity. Verify both rates on the day you transact.BOC rate board · CFETS central parity announcement
Emigration / inheritance property transfer eligibilityThe route in the PBOC Interim Measures applies to people who emigrated from mainland China or to foreign citizens who inherited assets in China. It does not on its face cover a US citizen’s own savings. Confirm eligibility before relying on it.PBOC Interim Measures, Art. 2
Conflict in the property-transfer approval thresholdArticle 10 of the Measures says applications of CNY 500,000 or less are approved by the local SAFE branch and larger ones go to head office; SAFE’s own press release describes the split as “below CNY 500,000” versus “CNY 500,000 or more”. The boundary case is therefore ambiguous.PBOC Interim Measures, Art. 10 · SAFE release
SAFE Detailed Rules Article 39 has been repealedThe up-to-CNY 1,000 fine on individuals that earlier versions of this site cited was deleted by SAFE circular 汇发〔2023〕8号. Do not rely on it; penalties now come from the PRC Foreign Exchange Administration Regulations.SAFE Detailed Rules (as amended)
Whether a US passport holder has a US$50,000 annual quotaThe quota in Article 2 covers settlement and purchases by domestic individuals; SAFE separately extends it to holders of a foreign permanent-resident ID card. A US passport alone is not shown by the verified pages to create a quota. Confirm with the bank before planning around it.SAFE Detailed Rules, Art. 2 · SAFE 2017
