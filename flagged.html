I-07FBAR penalty amounts were mislabelled as a 2026 adjustmentThe previous site labelled $16,536 / $165,353 as the '2026 adjustment'. I-08Two different Form 3520 gift thresholds were conflatedThe site presented $20,573 as the general foreign-gift threshold. It is the section 6039F threshold for gifts from foreign CORPORATIONS and PARTNERSHIPS (Rev. Proc. 2025-32 s.4.47). Gifts from a nonresident alien INDIVIDUAL or a foreign estate use a non-indexed 'more than $100,000' threshold (Form 3520 instructions, Who Must File item 4(a)).Split into two separate rows, U07 and U08, each with its own source.high
I-09Fabricated penalty ranges in the previous version of the sitepenalties.html stated fines of '30%-300%' under Art. 45 and '30%-500%' under Art. 39. Neither appears in State Council Order 532. Both articles read '处...30%以下的罚款；情节严重的，处...30%以上等值以下的罚款' — up to 30%, and in serious cases 30% up to 100% (等值 = the equivalent amount). A separate '1 to 5 times' figure belongs to Art. 46 and applies to unlicensed OPERATORS, not customers.Corrected against the verbatim statute (S02), with the Chinese text quoted in the penalty table so the correction is auditable.critical
I-10The USD 50,000 quota was misapplied to a US citizenThe previous index.html told a US citizen they are limited to USD 50,000 a year. Detailed Rules Art. 2 imposes the annual purchase total only on 境内个人. Under Order 532 Art. 52(2) a foreigner is a domestic individual only after one full year of continuous residence in China. A non-resident US citizen instead uses Art. 13 with tax vouchers, which states no numeric annual cap.Added an explicit classification step at the top of the decision guide, with both articles quoted.critical
I-11Repealed provision cited as current lawThe previous penalties page cited Detailed Rules Art. 39 (a fine of up to RMB 1,000 for individuals).
