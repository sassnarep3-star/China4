Policy

A value is printed only if it comes from an official source or from arithmetic on such a value, with the formula shown. Otherwise the cell is left blank and appears below. Every row of every table on the site carries a link to an official source; a blank cell is deliberate, not an oversight.

Retrieval log

All sources listed on the Sources page were retrieved on 2026-11-08. Retrieval confirms what the page said on that date; it does not guarantee the page will remain available, nor that a Chinese-language page and its English translation say precisely the same thing. Where SAFE publishes both, the Chinese text is authoritative — its own English pages say so — and the Chinese text was used for every number here.

What would change the answer

  • A change in the US$50,000 quota, or in Art. 13 of the Detailed Rules.
  • Your own residency history in China, and whether hukou was cancelled.
  • The source of the money: taxed salary, property, inheritance or gift.
  • Whether the funds have already been declared on prior US returns.

Each of those is a fact only you and your documents can supply — which is why this page, rather than a confident single number, is the honest conclusion.
