Tax and reporting obligations

Federal US analysis plus China tax rules; not a personalized tax opinion.

Core distinction

Moving previously reported principal is not a second income item, but underlying income/gain and CNY disposition can still matter. Reporting forms are not transfer taxes. Numeric personal tax remains blank without residence, income, basis, treaty and state facts.

IRS: International individual tax matters FAQ · US Code / GovInfo: 26 USC 988 (2024 edition) · State Taxation Administration: 中华人民共和国个人所得税法 (2018 amendment)

Legitimate ways to reduce cost and risk

  • Obtain simultaneous quotes for the same net USD destination amount, with identical fee allocation (SHA/OUR), expected credited amount, validity time and receiving-bank charges. Do not rank providers from a fee alone.
  • Use the actual lawful source route. Repeated salary documentation at the same bank can reduce preparation friction; eligible Wise/SkyRemit may reduce branch visits, but neither is proven cheapest without quotes.
  • Reconcile principal, interest and gains before conversion. Claim only eligible FTC/FEIE and treaty benefits; keep acquisition-date USD basis records for CNY lots.
  • For a real property sale, substantiate basis and reasonable expenses and test the actual five-year/sole-family-home relief. Do not create a sale or recharacterize income to obtain relief.
  • Avoid unnecessary double conversion and cash routes for large balances. A capped wire commission can make consolidated lawful transfers efficient, but do not split to evade controls.
  • File FBAR/8938/3520/105 correctly when triggered. Reporting compliance avoids penalties; it is not a tax deduction or a way to erase underlying tax.
  • Have a US–China adviser model federal, state, currency-gain, treaty-residence and any entity-reporting effects before a large distribution or sale.
