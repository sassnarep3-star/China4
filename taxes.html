US & China Taxes, Treaty Rules & Compliance

Preventing double taxation while staying compliant with PRC tax recordal, IRC §988 FX gain rules, FBAR/FATCA, Form 3520, and Form 8621.

Key Myth Debunked: Moving Your Own Money Is Not a New Tax

Simply converting or wiring your own already-taxed RMB savings from your Chinese bank account to your US bank account does not create a new US income tax event—nor does the 2026 OBBBA IRC §4475 1% remittance excise tax apply to inbound China-to-US transfers (IRS IR-2026-48). The only potential US tax on the conversion itself is an IRC §988 foreign currency exchange gain if the RMB appreciated in USD terms between receipt and conversion and the gain exceeds $200 (26 U.S.C. §988).

1984 US–China Income Tax Treaty In force (effective Jan 1, 1987). Dividends 10% cap, Interest 10% cap, Teachers/Researchers (Art. 19) & Students (Art. 20) relief; Saving Clause (Protocol ¶2) limits US citizen claims. IRC §988 Foreign Currency Gain / Loss Gain on disposing of nonfunctional currency (CNY→USD) is ordinary income; personal transactions exempt only if gain ≤$200 (§988(e)(2)). Personal FX losses are nondeductible. Record the USD value when you earned/received each RMB tranche (e.g., payroll date or interest credit). With Nov 2026 parity at ~6.7168 vs ~7.1–7.3 in 2024–2025, older RMB may show a taxable §988 currency gain when converted to USD! 26 U.S.C. §988 · IRS FX Practice Unit
FBAR (FinCEN Form 114) Mandatory if combined max balance of Chinese bank/Alipay/WeChat/brokerage accounts exceeds $10,000 at any moment in calendar year. Due Apr 15 (auto-extended Oct 15). File electronically via FinCEN BSA E-Filing for $0 tax—avoids $16,536 non-willful or $165,353+/50% willful fines. IRS FBAR · 2026 Fed. Reg.
FATCA Form 8938 Living in US: >$50k year-end or >$75k anytime (Single; $100k/$150k MFJ). Living Abroad: >$200k year-end or >$300k anytime (Single; $400k/$600k MFJ). Attached to Form 1040. Form 3520 (Foreign Gifts & Inheritances) Information-only return if receiving >$100,000 from a nonresident alien/estate in a year (or >$20,573 in 2026 from a foreign corporation/partnership). Bona fide foreign gifts/inheritances are $0 US income tax—but failing to file Form 3520 triggers a 5%/month penalty up to 25%! IRS Form 3520 · Rev. Proc. 2025-32
Form 8621 PFIC Trap (Chinese Funds / QDII / Yu’ebao) Chinese mutual funds, ETFs, QDII products, and certain money-market funds (e.g. Yu’ebao / wealth products) often classify as PFICs. Keep RMB in plain bank cash deposits (not fund wealth-management products) if planning to move to USD; PFICs suffer top marginal tax + compound interest. IRS Form 8621
