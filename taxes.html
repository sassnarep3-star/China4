A transfer and a filing requirement are different questions. Moving or closing the account does not erase earlier obligations.

US obligations

TopicRule / decisionOfficial source
Worldwide incomeUS citizens generally report worldwide income whether or not they live abroad. Interest, wages and other earnings require their own analysis.IRS Pub. 54
Foreign-currency movementThe wire of principal is not the same as earning income, but buying or selling nonfunctional currency can implicate section 988; determine basis, gain and applicable exceptions with an adviser.IRS FX guidance
FBARGenerally required when aggregate foreign financial account values exceed $10,000 at any time during the calendar year; filing is separate from the tax return.IRS FBAR
Form 8938Specified foreign assets may require Form 8938 attached to the tax return; thresholds vary with filing status and residence. IRS notes no Form 8938 if no income tax return is required.IRS FATCA
Foreign gift / bequestForm 3520 may be required when gifts or bequests from a nonresident alien individual or foreign estate exceed $100,000 in the year, including relevant related-person aggregation. It is an information report, not automatically a gift tax.IRS 3520 instructions
Foreign tax credit / earned income exclusionQualified foreign income taxes may be creditable subject to limitations. No credit for taxes on excluded foreign earned income.IRS foreign tax credit
Missed filingsReview the IRS streamlined procedures and eligibility; do not assume a particular program or penalty relief applies.IRS procedures
