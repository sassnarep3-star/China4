IDTopicRule / boundaryOfficial sources
C01ClassificationPBOC Art. 37 defines domestic individuals by PRC ID/military papers and foreign individuals by passport/HK/Macau/Taiwan travel documents; Decree 532 Art. 52 uses a separate one-year residence definition. Do not infer classification from nationality alone; use the bank's account/identity classification.[T02] [T04]
C02USD 50,000 quotaUSD50,000 is an annual convenience purchase/settlement quota, not a hard cap on genuine lawful current-account transactions; above it requires ID plus transaction-amount/source or purpose documents.[T01] [T02] [T03]
C03Foreign individual's RMB incomeA foreign individual with lawful current-account RMB income may purchase FX and remit with ID and supporting/tax documents; unused inward FX may be remitted back with ID.[T01] [T02]
C04Anti-splittingNo splitting, borrowed quotas, false documents or private swap channels. Hui Fa [2015] No. 49 imposes a watch list for the listing year and two following years.[T04] [T05] [T06]
C05Licensed channelsIndividual FX purchase/sale must use qualified banks or authorized exchange agencies; company/app/social-media/broker/underground-bank channels are unlawful. Virtual-currency-related exchange and settlement business is also prohibited.[T06] [T14]
C06Tax filing gateFor service-trade and other specified outbound payments, a single payment above USD50,000 requires tax filing; the unchanged amount standard is USD50,000, and for one contract with multiple payments filing occurs only when the first payment exceeds it.[T07] [T08]
C07FX-savings remittanceDomestic-individual FX-savings remittance ≤USD50,000/day uses ID; above that needs genuine current-account documents. Foreign-individual FX-savings remittance uses ID. ICBC's product page repeats these rules.[T01] [T26]
C08Cash-account conversionIf funds are in a foreign-currency cash account rather than a remittance account, ICBC and CCB describe a cash-to-remittance spread charged at the day's board rate; include it in a written quote.[T21] [T26]
C09Overseas ATMCNY100,000 per person per calendar year across own cards and CNY10,000 per card per day; exceeding the annual cap suspends current and next-year overseas cash withdrawals. Ordinary card consumption is unaffected and does not consume the USD50,000 convenience quota.[T09] [T10] [T11]
C10Cash and border reportingForeign-currency cash: ≤USD5,000 permit-free, USD5,000–10,000 bank permit, >USD10,000 generally prohibited except special SAFE permit. RMB cash limit is CNY20,000 per person per trip. Entering/leaving the US with more than USD10,000 in currency/monetary instruments requires FinCEN Form 105; group aggregation applies.[T12] [T48] [T46]
C11Social insuranceForeign employees participate in five insurances. On departure before pension age the personal account is retained; only a written application to terminate the social-insurance relation permits a one-time payment of the personal-account balance. The 2024 amendment changes eligibility-document names and annual overseas eligibility verification.[T35] [T36]
C12US tax/reportingUS citizens/resident aliens remain on worldwide-income filing rules; personal FX gain over USD200 is a capital gain; FTC uses Form 1116 and cannot apply to excluded income; FBAR aggregate foreign accounts >USD10,000 and Form 8938 have separate thresholds; FEIE requires a foreign tax home and residence/presence test.[T41] [T42] [T43] [T44] [T45] [T49]

03 · Execution

What a written quote must contain

1

Same-time inputs

Ask for the FX selling rate, commission, telegram, cash-to-remittance spread, full-arrival charge and expected final recipient amount at the same quote time.

[T16] [T20] [T21] [T22] [T31]

2

Route conditions

Confirm identity classification, lawful source-of-funds/tax documents, own-account destination, tax-filing number where applicable, and any channel fee.

[T01] [T02] [T07] [T28] [T29] [T33]

3

Downstream deductions

Ask explicitly about intermediary/receiving-bank deductions and SHA/BEN/OUR allocation. A 'free' bank fee is not an all-in quote.

[T16] [T22] [T26] [T31] [T32]
