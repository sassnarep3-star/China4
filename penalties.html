What violations actually cost

Every penalty read from State Council Order 532 and the governing notices, with the Chinese text quoted.

Correction to earlier versions of this site: it claimed fines of “30%–300%” and “30%–500%”. Neither figure exists in the statute. Arts. 39, 40 and 45 all read up to 30%, and in serious cases 30% up to 100% — 等值 means the equivalent amount, i.e. 100%. The “1 to 5 times” multiplier belongs to Art. 46 and applies to unlicensed operators, not customers. S02

Percentages below are of the illegal amount. “Serious circumstances” is a finding by SAFE, not a fixed threshold, so treat the upper tier as a real possibility rather than a remote one.

P07Order 532 Art. 46
未经批准擅自经营结汇、售汇业务 — operating an FX settlement/sale business without approval
Acting as an unlicensed FX dealer (the operator, not the customer)
Fixed amounts: Confiscation of illegal gains. Where illegal gains are RMB 500,000 or more: a fine of 1 to 5 TIMES the illegal gains. Where there are no illegal gains, or gains are under RMB 500,000: a fine of RMB 500,000 to RMB 2,000,000.
Serious cases: ordered to suspend business for rectification or have the business licence revoked. Criminal liability where a crime is constituted.
Chinese statutory text
未经批准擅自经营结汇、售汇业务的，由外汇管理机关责令改正，有违法所得的，没收违法所得，违法所得50万元以上的，并处违法所得1倍以上5倍以下的罚款；没有违法所得或者违法所得不足50万元的，处50万元以上200万元以下的罚款；情节严重的，由有关主管部门责令停业整顿或者吊销业务许可证；构成犯罪的，依法追究刑事责任。
——S02
P08Order 532 Art. 48
申报、单证、账户、登记及配合检查违规 — reporting, document, account, registration and inspection-cooperation violations
Failing to make balance-of-payments declarations, submitting untrue documents, breaching FX account or registration rules, obstructing inspection
Fixed amounts: Order to rectify and a warning. Institutions: fine up to RMB 300,000. INDIVIDUALS: fine up to RMB 50,000.
This is the flat-cap provision most relevant to an individual's paperwork failures, as distinct from the percentage fines on the amount moved.
Chinese statutory text
有下列情形之一的，由外汇管理机关责令改正，给予警告，对机构可以处30万元以下的罚款，对个人可以处5万元以下的罚款
——S02
P09Huifa [2015] No. 49 item 2
分拆结售汇 / 出借或借用他人额度 — splitting, lending or borrowing annual quota
Quota splitting; lending your own USD 50,000 allowance; borrowing someone else's
Fixed amounts: Not a fine in itself. Lending your quota: first offence draws a risk-warning letter, a repeat draws watch-listing. Borrowing another's quota: immediate watch-listing.
Watch-list period is the year of listing PLUS the following two consecutive years. During that period the person cannot use the convenient ID-only channel and must present documentary proof of the transaction amount at the counter for every purchase or settlement. Any underlying evasion or arbitrage is separately fined under Art. 39 or Art. 40.
——S03 S02

P11Yinfa [2021] No. 237 items 1(2), 1(3), 1(4)
虚拟货币相关业务活动属于非法金融活动
Using crypto or stablecoins to move value out of China
Fixed amounts: No percentage schedule is given in this notice. Fiat-to-crypto exchange business is classified as an illegal financial activity that is strictly prohibited and to be banned; overseas exchanges serving mainland residents over the internet are likewise illegal financial activity.
Where a crime is constituted, criminal liability is pursued. Related civil acts contrary to public order and good morals are void and the resulting losses are borne by the participant. Banks and payment institutions may not provide accounts, transfers, settlement, collateral or insurance for such activity.
