Penalty breakdown

Separate transfer fees/FX friction from actual taxes and reporting penalties.

Penalties are not prices

Lawful transfer budgeting should include FX friction, published fees, routing/receiving charges, professional preparation and actual tax. Statutory penalties are sanctions for violations or reporting failures, not expected costs of a lawful route.
