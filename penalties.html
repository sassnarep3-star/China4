Published bank tariff: one component, not the total

Bank of China’s 2025 personal-services price list states a standard cross-border remittance charge of 0.1% of the remittance amount, minimum CNY 50 and maximum CNY 260, plus CNY 150 for an international telegraphic transfer. The bank lists electronic-channel discounts separately and actual foreign bank charges may apply. This is a historical published tariff, not a verified current quote or guarantee of service for this account. The bank’s tariff has a separate full-amount-arrival service charge in some cases.

For one qualifying international wire, its published base sending-charge formula is min(CNY 260, max(CNY 50, 0.001 × remittance value in CNY)) + CNY 150. Confirm the bank’s exchange basis and any discount. This excludes the exchange spread, foreign banks’ charges, and any tax. Bank of China tariff PDF, personal services item 210105008 and notes.

Compare net proceeds

Net USD = quoted USD after conversion − sending fee − intermediary deductions − receiving fee. If a fee is charged in CNY, account for it before conversion rather than subtracting it again in USD. Taxes and penalties must be analyzed separately; do not subtract hypothetical fines as if they were a normal service charge. Actual values are unknown.
