The Net-USD Formula (Avoid Double-Counting)

Net USD Delivered = (CNY Converted ÷ Executable Sell Rate) − USD-Denominated Wire/OUR Fee − Correspondent Deduction − US Bank Incoming Fee (or if commission/cable is paid separately in CNY, convert total CNY spent at the executable rate). Underlying PRC/US income taxes, §988 FX gains, and statutory fines are separate legal liabilities—not wire surcharges.
