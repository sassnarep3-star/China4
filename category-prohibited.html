Underground money changer / matched RMB-USD swap prohibited

Excluded. Paying RMB domestically for USD abroad through an unauthorized matcher is not a verified lawful route and creates enforcement/fraud risk.

Eligibility: None for the proposed workaround.

Limits: Not applicable; do not use.

Pros

  • None that outweigh the risk

Cons

  • Illegal FX trading/evasion exposure
  • Confiscation/fines/criminal risk
  • Counterparty fraud and no recourse

CNY-to-crypto-to-USD P2P workaround prohibited

Excluded. Current PBOC 2026 No. 42 treats mainland virtual-currency-related business as illegal financial activity and restricts overseas services to mainland subjects.

Eligibility: None for the proposed exchange/business workaround.

Limits: Not applicable; do not use.

Pros

  • None that outweigh the risk

Cons

  • Illegal financial activity exposure
  • Exchange/counterparty fraud
  • US tax complexity does not make it lawful

Borrowing family/friend quotas or splitting to evade controls prohibited

Excluded. Using another person's quota or splitting transactions to evade authenticity/quota controls is not a lawful planning technique.

Eligibility: None for evasion. Genuine family support must be evaluated on its own facts.
