How to use this page

No route, cost or tax conclusion is reasonable until identity, source of funds, Chinese tax history and US reporting history are known. This page lists every decision question, what the official rule already answers, what is still unknown for this person, and the exact step that discovers it. Unknowns stay blank until discovered — they are never guessed. [S1] [S2] [S32]

What this page is not

This is general research, not individualized legal, tax or investment advice. Eligibility follows the source and character of funds, the identity used at the bank, tax residence and transaction purpose. Obtain a written all-in quote and qualified cross-border tax review before execution. [S32] [S4]

01 · Questions and answers

Fourteen questions before any decision

18 procedures →
Every row links the primary evidence for the known answer. Unknowns are personal facts or live quotes and stay blank until discovered by the stated step.
QuestionWhat the official rule already answersStill unknown for this personHow to discover itOfficial sources
Q1. What identity does the Chinese bank have on file?SAFE/PBOC measures define a passport-holding foreign citizen as a foreign individual; domestic versus foreign classification controls quota treatment.The bank's recorded classification for this account.Ask the bank in writing for its recorded classification; bring your passport; keep the reply.[S2] [S1]
Q2. What is the lawful source of each CNY deposit?After-tax current-account income, existing foreign-currency savings, unused prior-conversion RMB, inheritance and property proceeds each follow different rules.The actual source and character of the balance.Reconstruct the deposit trail tranche by tranche: contracts, payslips, statements; label each tranche before choosing a route.[S1] [S20] [S21]
Q3. Has Chinese individual income tax been paid on the income?A foreign individual's lawful current-account income purchase requires amount-linked supporting documents including tax vouchers.Your Chinese tax-payment history.Obtain tax-payment records from the tax authority or individual income tax app; keep the receipts with the transfer file.[S1] [S11]
Q4. Is the balance CNY needing conversion, or already USD?Existing foreign-currency savings can be wired without a CNY conversion; a CNY purchase follows the foreign-individual income rules.The currency composition of the account.Check a bank statement showing the currency split; do not assume the whole balance needs conversion.[S1] [S4]
Q5. Does the USD 50,000 convenience quota apply to me?The annual convenience purchase quota is written for domestic individuals; genuine documented current-account needs can exceed it and are not limited to ID-only handling.Your classification from Q1.Confirm classification with the bank first; never assume ID-only permission or borrow another person's quota.[S1] [S46] [S19]
Q6. What is the all-in executable quote?The BOC wire tariff formula is published, but the execution FX rate is live and dated; the modeled matrices are not executable quotes.The live rate, commission, telecom charge, correspondent deduction and net USD credit.Request a written all-in quote (see the discovery checklist below) and compare routes on the same net USD received.[S4] [S5] [S48]
Q7. What will intermediaries or correspondents deduct?The published tariff does not include foreign-bank charges; BOC prices an optional full-arrival service that shifts the deduction to the sender.The actual deduction on this corridor for this transfer.Ask the bank what deduction applies; compare standard versus full-arrival pricing inside the written quote.[S4]
Q8. What will my US bank charge to receive the wire?Published examples exist (for example BofA and Chase Secure Checking inbound fees, and Capital One's tiered schedule), but each depends on the account type.The fee schedule for your own US account.Check your US bank's incoming-wire fee page for your exact account type before execution.[S42] [S43] [S44]
Q9. What is the US tax character of the money?US citizens are generally taxed on worldwide income; a personal foreign-currency gain is excluded unless more than USD 200, then reported as a capital gain; basis is a personal fact.Acquisition date and rate, income history, tax residence and basis.Reconstruct basis from records and obtain qualified cross-border tax review; the transfer amount alone cannot establish tax owed.[S32] [S38] [S47]
Q10. Have I met FBAR, Form 8938 and Form 3520 duties?FBAR applies above USD 10,000 aggregate at any time in the year; Form 8938 has separate thresholds; Form 3520 covers specified gifts, bequests and trusts, not moving your own savings.Your prior-year filing history.Review prior returns and FBAR filings; if eligible, evaluate the streamlined procedures rather than silently back-filing.[S25] [S27] [S28] [S36] [S37]
Q11. Am I eligible for Wise or SkyRemit?Wise from CNY requires a non-mainland-Chinese sender who has worked in China, a valid tax record from the last 3 years, and an own-account recipient; per-transfer caps are CNY 300,000 (bank app) and CNY 50,000 (Alipay).Your eligibility and overall document-based limit.Complete Wise verification with tax records; compare only a live same-net-payout quote, never the 2024 examples.[S11] [S12] [S13]
Q12. Am I carrying cash or using an ATM?RMB cash is subject to a per-trip limit; separately, US reporting generally applies to more than USD 10,000 in currency or monetary instruments. Overseas ATM withdrawals have annual and daily limits. Declaration does not automatically authorize exceeding a cash limit.Your travel and ATM plan.Check the cash and ATM limits before travel; do not treat cash or ATM as a USD 400,000 transfer route.[S49] [S50] [S51] [S9]
Q13. Is any part a gift, inheritance, property or investment proceeds?Qualifying emigration property, inheritance, property-sale and direct-investment proceeds each have their own application and evidence route.The character of each tranche.Gather the estate, property or investment documents and apply through the bank for that specific route.[S20] [S21] [S22]
Q14. What records must I keep?Retaining FX records, fee receipts and evidence of the final credit helps reconstruct the transaction. If an FBAR is required, account maximum values are relevant to the filing.The records available for each transaction and the applicable reporting obligations.Retain the complete chain from income to final credit and reconcile it against the modeled cost.[S1] [S4] [S25]
