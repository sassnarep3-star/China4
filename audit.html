A-01index.html, methods.html row 1A US citizen is limited to converting USD 50,000 per person per year.Misapplied. Detailed Rules Art. 2 imposes the annual total on settlement (结汇) for all individuals but on purchase (购汇) only for 境内个人. Order 532 Art. 52(2) defines 境内个人 as Chinese citizens plus foreigners continuously resident for one full year. A US citizen below that threshold is 境外个人 and converts under Art. 13 with tax vouchers, which states no annual numeric cap.
Action: Added an explicit classification step; split the routes by individual status.
criticalS01 S02
A-02penalties.html section B, index.htmlIllegal FX trading carries a fine of 30%-300%.Fabricated. Art. 45 reads 处违法金额30%以下的罚款；情节严重的，处违法金额30%以上等值以下的罚款 — up to 30%, and in serious cases 30% up to 100%. 等值 means the equivalent amount, i.e. 100%, not 300%.
Action: Corrected, with the Chinese statutory text quoted in the table so the correction is auditable.

A-03penalties.html section BEvasion of FX controls carries a fine of 30%-500%.Fabricated. Art. 39 uses the identical formula: up to 30%, serious cases 30% up to 100%. The '1 to 5 times' multiplier the site may have been reaching for belongs to Art. 46 and applies to unlicensed operators, not customers.
Action: Corrected; Art. 46 published as a separate row scoped to operators.

A-10taxes.htmlThere is no US tax on converting currency.Wrong as a blanket statement. IRC 988(c)(1)(C)(i) makes any disposition of nonfunctional currency a section 988 transaction, and (ii) expressly covers foreign-currency bank deposits. Gain is ordinary under 988(a)(1)(A). The 988(e)(2) exclusion is gain-only and switches off entirely once the gain exceeds $200.
Action: Added rows T02-T05 with the statutory text quoted, including the cliff behaviour of the $200 exclusion.
