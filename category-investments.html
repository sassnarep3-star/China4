Overseas/HK account opening witness service — supporting only supporting only

BOC publishes a pre-departure overseas account-opening witness service. Opening an account does not itself authorize CNY conversion or outbound remittance.

Eligibility: Bank customer meeting the overseas institution's account-opening/KYC rules.

Limits: Account opening only; transfer still needs a lawful source route. BOC witness fee is max CNY 400/account, charged to the委托方 in the tariff.

Pros

  • May prepare a receiving account
  • Published BOC witness fee cap

Cons

  • Not a transfer route
  • Overseas bank fees/KYC separate
  • Cannot bypass source controls

Wealth Management Connect Southbound — closed-loop investment closed loop

Eligible GBA mainland investors can invest through paired mainland/HK accounts in RMB; principal/proceeds return to the paired mainland account, not freely to a US personal account.

Eligibility: Eligible mainland investor in the Greater Bay Area scheme; paired dedicated accounts and quota management.

Limits: Closed-loop RMB via CIPS; offshore conversion only inside the dedicated investment account; return route is the paired mainland remittance account.

Pros

  • Regulated cross-boundary investment channel
  • Offshore product/currency options inside the scheme

Cons

  • Not a free USD disposal route
  • Eligibility and quota restrictions
  • Investment risk and scheme fees unverified

QDII product — institutional investment channel, not personal remittance closed loop

Domestic individuals may invest through qualified domestic institutional investors, but QDII is an investment product channel, not a verified route to dispose of USD freely in a US personal account.

Eligibility: Investor in an approved QDII product; institution holds quota and custody arrangements.

Limits: Product-specific subscription/redemption and return-account rules; no verified free US-account disposal. Do not use as a remittance workaround.

Pros

  • Regulated overseas investment access

Cons

  • Investment risk
  • Product fees/return rules unverified
  • Not a personal remittance route
