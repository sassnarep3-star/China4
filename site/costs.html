Practical read: for legitimately taxed salary, the conversion itself usually moves at the cost of bank fees plus spread only. The painful scenarios are exactly two: (1) the income was never taxed in China — then IIT (3%–45%) must be settled before conversion; (2) someone tells you to dodge documentation via underground channels — then penalties depend on the conduct and circumstances, and may include a fine or criminal case.
